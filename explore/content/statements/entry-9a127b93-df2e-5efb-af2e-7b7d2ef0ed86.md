::: theorem result:gauss-radau-quadrature-uniqueness name="Uniqueness of the Radau rule"
If a finite measure $\nu$ carried by $c$ and at most $k$
further points matches the first $2k + 1$ moments of a finite measure $\mu$ supported in
$[a, b]$, $c \in \{a, b\}$, then $\nu$ is the Radau rule of $\mu$ with prescribed node $c$ and

Note: $k$ free nodes. The source locator requires checking; the comparison is among rules with positive weights.
:::
