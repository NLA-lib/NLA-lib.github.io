::: theorem result:schatten-holder name="Hölder's inequality for the trace"
For conjugate exponents, $1/q + 1/r = 1$,
$$\lvert \operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{B}) \rvert
  \le \|\mathbf{A}\|_{S_q} \|\mathbf{B}\|_{S_r}\,.$$
:::
