::: theorem result:low-rank-interlacing name="Interlacing under a low-rank perturbation, upper"
For Hermitian $\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$
with $\operatorname{rank} \mathbf{B} \le r$ and $k + r < \lvert n \rvert$,
$\lambda_{k+r}(\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}) \le \lambda_k(\mathbf{A})$.

:::
