::: theorem result:weyl-inequality name="Weyl's inequality"
For Hermitian $\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$ and $i + j < \lvert n \rvert$,
$\lambda_{i+j}(\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}) \le \lambda_i(\mathbf{A}) + \lambda_j(\mathbf{B})$,
eigenvalues non-increasing and indexed from $0$.
The cited theorem is stated over $\mathbb{C}$ with eigenvalues in increasing order.
:::
