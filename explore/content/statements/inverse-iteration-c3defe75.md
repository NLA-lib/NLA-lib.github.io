::: definition name="Inverse iteration"
Choose a shift $\mu$ such that $\mathbf{A}-\mu\mathbf{I}$ is invertible. At each step solve $(\mathbf{A}-\mu\mathbf{I})\mathbf{y}_{j+1}=\mathbf{x}_j$ and normalize $\mathbf{y}_{j+1}$. Fixed-shift inverse iteration and a changing Rayleigh-quotient shift have different convergence hypotheses.
:::
