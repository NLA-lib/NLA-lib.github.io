::: theorem result:filtered-range-finder-bound name="Filtered range finder: expected error"
for a real function $g$, an orthonormal frame
$\mathbf{W}$ invariant under $\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}$,
$\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{W} = \mathbf{W}\operatorname{diag}(\mathbf{d})$, with
$\lvert g(\mathbf{d}_i) \rvert \ge c > 0$ for every $i$, and an orthonormal frame $\mathbf{Z}$ whose
range contains that of $\mathbf{W}$ and with $\mathbf{Z}^{\mathsf{H}}\boldsymbol{\Omega}$ of full row
rank,
$$\|\mathbf{A} - \mathsf{rsvd}(\mathbf{A},
g(\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A})\boldsymbol{\Omega})\|_{\mathsf{F}}^2
\le \|\mathbf{A} - \mathbf{A}\mathbf{W}\mathbf{W}^{\mathsf{H}}\|_{\mathsf{F}}^2 +
c^{-2}\|\mathbf{M}\|_{\mathsf{F}}^2\,,$$
with $\mathbf{B} = \mathbf{A}g(\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A})$ and the sketch residual
$\mathbf{M} = (\mathbf{B} - \mathbf{B}\mathbf{Z}\mathbf{Z}^{\mathsf{H}})\boldsymbol{\Omega}
(\mathbf{Z}^{\mathsf{H}}\boldsymbol{\Omega})^{+}$. The Gram form, in every unitarily invariant norm, is
`NLAlib.SymmetricGauge.matrixNorm_conjTranspose_mul_self_sub_rsvd_cfc_le`.
The statement is a specialization of the cited result at $p = 2$.
:::
