::: theorem result:gauss-quadrature-uniqueness name="Uniqueness of the Gauss rule"
if a finite measure $\nu$ carried by a set of at most $k$
points matches the first $2k$ moments of a finite measure $\mu$ supported in $[a, b]$, then $\nu$
is the $k$-point Gauss rule of $\mu$. The source locator requires checking. The comparison is with interpolatory rules having $k$
distinct nodes and positive weights; this statement allows any measure on at most $k$ points.
:::
