::: theorem result:pcg-finite-termination name="Finite termination of PCG"
For positive definite $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{M}$ and
$\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_\star = \mathbf{b}$, PCG reaches the solution once $q$ is at least the grade
of $\mathbf{M}\mathbf{r}_0$ for $\mathbf{M}\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{r}_0 = \mathbf{b} -
\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_0$:
$\mathsf{pcgIterate}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{M}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{x}_0, q) = \mathbf{x}_\star$.


Note: A projection method onto an invariant subspace containing the right side is exact. The cited Proposition 6.1 gives invariance of the Krylov space from the grade onward, and §9.2.1 treats PCG as CG on the split system.
:::
