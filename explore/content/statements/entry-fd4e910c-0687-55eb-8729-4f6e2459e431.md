::: theorem result:psd-cone-self-dual name="Self-duality of the positive semidefinite cone"
A Hermitian matrix is positive
semidefinite exactly when its trace pairing with every positive semidefinite matrix is
nonnegative: $\mathbf{0} \preceq \mathbf{A}$ iff $0 \le \operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B})$
for all $\mathbf{0} \preceq \mathbf{B}$.
:::
