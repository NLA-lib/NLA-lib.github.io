::: theorem result:exponential-kernel name="The entrywise exponential of a positive semidefinite matrix is positive definite exactly when its rows are distinct"
for real positive semidefinite $\mathbf{A}$, the matrix with entries
$e^{\mathbf{A}_{i,j}}$ is positive definite iff no two rows of $\mathbf{A}$ are equal.

:::
