::: theorem result:operator-capacity-scaling-identities name="Operator capacity under invertible scaling"
For a positive real-linear map on
complex $n$ by $n$ matrices, $n>0$, scale the map $\Phi$ by invertible matrices
$\mathbf{B},\mathbf{C}$ to obtain
$$\Psi(\mathbf{X})=\mathbf{B}\Phi(\mathbf{C}\mathbf{X}\mathbf{C}^{\mathsf{H}})
\mathbf{B}^{\mathsf{H}}.$$
Then $\operatorname{cap}(\Psi)=\lvert\det\mathbf{B}\rvert^2
\lvert\det\mathbf{C}\rvert^2\operatorname{cap}(\Phi)$.
This includes maps of zero capacity. Proposition 2.7 treats completely positive maps; this statement extends the identity to positive
real-linear maps.
:::
