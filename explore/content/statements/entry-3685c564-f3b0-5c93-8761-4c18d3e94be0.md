::: theorem result:gmres-polynomial-bound name="GMRES beats every residual polynomial"
Let $\Bbbk$ be the real or complex field, $\mathbf{A}$ a square matrix over $\Bbbk$,
$\mathbf{b}$ a right-hand side and $\mathbf{x}_0$ an initial vector, and set
$\mathbf{r}_0=\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_0$. For the $q$-th [GMRES
iterate](/explore/algorithms/gmres/) $\mathbf{x}_q$ in exact arithmetic, with
$q\in\mathbb{N}$, and every $p\in\Bbbk[X]$ with
$\deg p\le q$ and $p(0)=1$,

$$\|\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_q\|_2\le\|p(\mathbf{A})\mathbf{r}_0\|_2.$$
:::
