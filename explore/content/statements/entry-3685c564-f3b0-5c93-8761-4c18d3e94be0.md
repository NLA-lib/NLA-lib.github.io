::: theorem result:gmres-polynomial-bound name="GMRES beats every residual polynomial"
for every $\mathbf{A}$ and $p \in \Bbbk[X]$ with
$\deg p \le q$, $p(0) = 1$, $\|\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{A}\,\mathsf{gmresIterate}(\mathbf{A},
\mathbf{b}, \mathbf{x}_0, q)\| \le \|p(\mathbf{A})\mathbf{r}_0\|$.
:::
