::: theorem result:hadamard-determinant-inequality name="Hadamard's inequality"
The determinant of a positive semidefinite matrix is at most the
product of its diagonal entries:
$\operatorname{Re} \det \mathbf{A} \le \prod_i \operatorname{Re} \mathbf{A}_{i,i}$.

:::
