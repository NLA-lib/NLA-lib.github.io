::: theorem result:cg-finite-termination name="Finite termination"
Let $g$ be the dimension of the [Krylov space](/explore/statements/615008ca-f5db-5cff-ab6b-b07f178cbd05/) generated by the initial residual. Once $k\ge g$, the mathematical iterate satisfies $\mathbf{x}_k=\mathbf{x}_\star$. In dimension $n$, at most $n$ exact-arithmetic steps suffice.
:::
