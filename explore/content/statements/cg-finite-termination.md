::: theorem result:cg-finite-termination name="Finite termination"
Let $\mathbb{K}\in\{\mathbb{R},\mathbb{C}\}$ and let $\mathbf{A}\in\mathbb{K}^{n\times n}$ be Hermitian positive definite, let $\mathbf{b}\in\mathbb{K}^n$, and suppose $\mathbf{x}_\star$ solves $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_\star=\mathbf{b}$. Starting from $\mathbf{x}_0$, define the initial residual $\mathbf{r}_0=\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_0$. Let $g$ be the dimension of the Krylov space generated by $\mathbf{r}_0$ and $\mathbf{A}$, equivalently the grade of $\mathbf{r}_0$ with respect to $\mathbf{A}$. For every nonnegative integer $k\ge g$, the exact-arithmetic [conjugate-gradient iterate](/explore/algorithms/conjugate-gradient/) $\mathbf{x}_k$ equals $\mathbf{x}_\star$. In dimension $n$, at most $n$ exact-arithmetic steps suffice.
:::
