::: theorem result:gauss-quadrature-remainder name="The Gauss quadrature remainder"
For a finite measure $\mu$ supported in $[a, b]$,
$a < b$, $k > 0$, and $f$ continuous on $[a, b]$ and $C^{2k}$ on $(a, b)$, there is
$\xi \in (a, b)$ with
$$\int f\,d\mu - \int f\,d\,\mathsf{gaussQuadrature}(\mu, k) =
\frac{f^{(2k)}(\xi)}{(2k)!}\int\pi_k^2\,d\mu\,,$$
$\pi_k$ the monic orthogonal polynomial of degree $k$. When $\mu$ has at most $k$ support
points both sides are $0$. The source locators require checking; the cited result assumes $f \in C^{2k}[a, b]$.
:::
