::: theorem result:singular-value-interlacing name="Interlacing for deleted columns"
If the injective $c : p \to n$ selects $\lvert p \rvert$
of the $\lvert p \rvert + d$ columns of $\mathbf{A}$, then
$\sigma_{k+d}(\mathbf{A}) \le \sigma_k(\mathbf{A}')$ for every $k$, where $\mathbf{A}'$ is
`A.submatrix id c`.
:::
