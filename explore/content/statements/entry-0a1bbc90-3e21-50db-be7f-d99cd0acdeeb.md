::: theorem result:tape-independent-draws name="Consecutive draws are independent"
If the tape $x$ is an i.i.d. sequence with a
probability law $\nu$, the $N \times k$ matrix drawn from position $0$ and the $N \times \ell$
matrix drawn after it, from position $Nk$, are independent.

:::
