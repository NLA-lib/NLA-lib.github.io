::: definition name="Orthogonal projection"
If $\mathbf{Q}$ has [orthonormal columns](/explore/statements/adb72f11-3503-58f9-89c4-98a60c4f7a40/), then $\mathbf{P}=\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{Q}^{\mathsf{H}}$ is the orthogonal projector onto their range: $\mathbf{P}^{\mathsf{H}}=\mathbf{P}$ and $\mathbf{P}^2=\mathbf{P}$. For any vector $\mathbf{x}$, the projected vector lies in the subspace and the residual $\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{P}\mathbf{x}$ is orthogonal to it. Principal angles compare two subspaces rather than particular choices of basis.
:::
