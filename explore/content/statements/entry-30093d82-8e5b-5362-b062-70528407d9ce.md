::: theorem result:weyl-singular-values-product name="Singular values of a product"
$\sigma_{i+j}(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}) \le \sigma_i(\mathbf{A}) \, \sigma_j(\mathbf{B})$ for all
indices from $0$.
:::
