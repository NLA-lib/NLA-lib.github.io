::: theorem result:newton-schulz-sign-perturbed-step-keeps-sign name="The perturbed Newton–Schulz step keeps the sign"
Lemma 2.3 (first part): if
$\lvert \xi \rvert \le u$ and $u < \lvert x \rvert < \sqrt3 - (\sqrt3 - 1)u$, then $g(x) + \xi$
has the sign of $x$, that is $x(g(x) + \xi) > 0$. The displayed implication does not require $u \le 3/16$.

Note: The cited source assumes $u \le 3/16$.
:::
