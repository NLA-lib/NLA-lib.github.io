::: theorem result:inner-product-forward-error name="Forward error of an inner product"
The bound uses Higham’s equation (3.5) with the factor from Carson et al.’s equation (3).
Under the standard model with $0 \le u < 1$,
$$\Bigl\lvert \sum_{k<n} x_k y_k - s_n \Bigr\rvert \le \gamma_n \sum_{k<n}
\lvert x_k \rvert \lvert y_k \rvert\,, \qquad \gamma_n = (1 - u)^{-n} - 1\,.$$
:::
