::: theorem result:lanczos-tridiagonal name="Tridiagonal compression"
Let $\mathbf{A}$ be Hermitian and let $\mathbf{Q}_k$ be a graded Krylov basis. Then $\mathbf{Q}_k^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q}_k$ is tridiagonal. This structure is the mathematical reason that Lanczos can use a short recurrence.
:::
