::: theorem result:lie-product-formula name="The Lie product formula"
for matrices:
$(\exp(\mathbf{H}/k)\exp(\mathbf{K}/k))^k \to \exp(\mathbf{H} + \mathbf{K})$ as $k \to \infty$,
for any two square matrices over an `RCLike` field.

:::
