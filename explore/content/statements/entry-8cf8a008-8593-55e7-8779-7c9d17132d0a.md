::: theorem result:best-approximation-existence name="A best approximation exists"
for $s$ compact and $f$ continuous on $s$ there is a real
polynomial $p$ of degree at most $n$ with $\lvert f(x) - p(x) \rvert \le E_n(f; s)$ on $s$.
This is how a bound stated for an arbitrary approximant is restated with $E_n$.
The cited results treat $[-1, 1]$ or an interval; this statement treats any compact set, including the empty set.
:::
