::: theorem result:leverage-sampling-osi name="Leverage-score sampling: subspace injection"
Row sampling under leverage domination
$\ell_i(\mathbf{U}) \le L p_i$ is a subspace injection for $\operatorname{range}\mathbf{U}$ with
constant $1 - \varepsilon$ and failure probability $\delta$, for $\varepsilon > 0$, $\delta > 0$
and <!-- value samplingInjectionDim \operatorname{rank}\mathbf{U} L \varepsilon \delta -->
$\lvert k \rvert \ge 2 L \log(\operatorname{rank}\mathbf{U}/\delta)/\varepsilon^2$ samples
(`NLAlib.samplingInjectionDim U.rank L ε δ`). Woodruff's cited Theorem 17 is the two-sided form for orthonormal columns, with more than $144 d \log(2d/\delta)/(\beta\varepsilon^2)$ samples, $d = \operatorname{rank}\mathbf{U}$ and $L = d/\beta$. The cited Tropp lower tail is weakened and solved for the sample size. Asymptotic form:
`NLAlib.exists_forall_hasSubspaceInjection_of_hasLaw_samplingSketch`.
:::
