::: theorem result:gaussian-interpolation name="Gaussian interpolation"
(Kahane's inequality): for centred Gaussian vectors $\mathbf{g}$,
$\mathbf{g}'$ in $\mathbb{R}^\iota$ and $F$ of class $C^2$ with Hessian bounded by $C$, if
$$\sum_{i,j} \bigl(\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{g}'_i \mathbf{g}'_j] -
\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{g}_i \mathbf{g}_j]\bigr) \, \partial_i \partial_j F(\mathbf{x}) \ge 0$$
for every $\mathbf{x}$, then $\mathbb{E} F(\mathbf{g}) \le \mathbb{E} F(\mathbf{g}')$.
The cited derivation gives an identity along the interpolating path $\sqrt{t}\, \mathbf{g} + \sqrt{1 - t}\, \mathbf{g}'$; here we use its inequality conclusion for $\mathbf{g}$ and $\mathbf{g}'$ on different probability spaces.
:::
