::: theorem result:general-form-tikhonov-gsvd name="Uniqueness on the penalty kernel"
For normalized GSVD coordinates and trivial
common kernel, every best data fit on the penalty kernel is the infinite-parameter endpoint.
Neither matrix individually needs full column rank; empty dimensions are included.

Note: Derived here using strict convexity of the Euclidean norm; no printed source for this formulation.
:::
