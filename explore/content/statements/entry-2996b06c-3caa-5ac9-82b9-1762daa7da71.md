::: theorem result:polar-newton-iteration name="Polar Newton closed form"
In a compact SVD with strictly positive singular values,
the actual kth iterate retains the singular directions and applies the scalar Cayley formula.
For $c_i=(\sigma_i-1)/(\sigma_i+1)$ the new coefficients are
$$\frac{1+c_i^{2^k}}{1-c_i^{2^k}}.$$
:::
