::: theorem result:polynomial-spectral-bound name="The norm of a polynomial in a Hermitian matrix"
For a Hermitian matrix $\mathbf{A}$ and real polynomial $p$, if $c \ge 0$ and
$\lvert p(x) \rvert \le c$ for every $x$ in the spectrum of $\mathbf{A}$, then
$\|p(\mathbf{A})\|_2 \le c$. The norm is at most the largest value of $\lvert p\rvert$
on the eigenvalues of $\mathbf{A}$.

:::
