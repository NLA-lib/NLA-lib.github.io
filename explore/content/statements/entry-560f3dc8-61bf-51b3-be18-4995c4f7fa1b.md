::: theorem result:normal-spectral-theorem name="The spectral theorem for normal matrices"
A complex matrix is normal exactly when it is
unitarily diagonalisable,
$\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{U} \operatorname{diag}(\mathbf{d}) \mathbf{U}^{\mathsf{H}}$ for a unitary
$\mathbf{U}$ and a complex $\mathbf{d}$..
:::
