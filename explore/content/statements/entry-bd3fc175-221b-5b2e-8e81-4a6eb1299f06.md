::: theorem result:srht-ose name="The SRHT OSE"
for a bounded orthonormal $\mathbf{F}$ with constant $\beta$,
$0 \le \varepsilon \le 1$ and $t \ge 0$, the SRHT is an oblivious subspace embedding for
dimension $d$ with constants $1 \mp \varepsilon$ and failure probability
$$2 d \exp\Bigl(-\frac{\varepsilon^2 \lvert k \rvert}{3 \beta (d + 2\sqrt{d t} + 2t)}\Bigr) +
  \lvert n \rvert e^{-t}\,.$$
The first cited result has
$r = \Omega(\varepsilon^{-2} \log d\, (\sqrt{d} + \sqrt{\log n})^2)$ rows, probability $0.99$,
real Walsh, $n$ a power of two; the second gives
$d = O(k \log k)$ for any SRTT; the third treats the SRFT with complex signs, $0.40 \le \sigma_{\min}$,
$\sigma_{\max} \le 1.48$, failure $O(1/k)$; the fourth treats Walsh–Hadamard with orthonormal
columns: the same constants at $4(\sqrt{d} + \sqrt{8 \log(d \lvert n \rvert)})^2 \log d$ rows, and
$1/\sqrt{6} \le \sigma_{\min}$, $\sigma_{\max} \le \sqrt{13/6}$ outside probability $3/d$. Here
any field, any $\mathbf{F}$ with entry bound $\beta$, the tail in natural parameters. For
$\lvert n \rvert < \lvert k \rvert$ the law is $0$, so $\mu = 0$.
:::
