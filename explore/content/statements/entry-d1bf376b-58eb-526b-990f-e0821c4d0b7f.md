::: theorem result:pcg-orthogonality name="PCG: orthogonal residuals and conjugate directions"
For positive definite $\mathbf{A}$ and
$\mathbf{M}$: $\mathbf{p}_j^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{p}_k = 0$ for $j < k$.
Saad shows that the PCG directions are
$\mathbf{L}^{-\mathsf{H}}$ times the CG directions of the split system
$\mathbf{L}^{-1}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{L}^{-\mathsf{H}}$,
$\mathbf{M}^{-1} = \mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^{\mathsf{H}}$, with the same $\mathbf{A}$-inner products,
and Hestenes and Stiefel prove that those directions are conjugate; both results are over $\mathbb{R}$.
:::
