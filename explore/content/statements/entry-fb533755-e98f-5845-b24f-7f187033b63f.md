::: theorem result:kantorovich-inequality name="The Kantorovich inequality with the condition number"
For positive definite
$\mathbf{A}$ and every vector $\mathbf{x}$, with $\kappa = \|\mathbf{A}\|_2 \|\mathbf{A}^{+}\|_2$
(`NLAlib.pseudoConditionNumber`; $\mathbf{A}$ is invertible, so this is the classical
$\kappa(\mathbf{A}) = \|\mathbf{A}\|_2 \|\mathbf{A}^{-1}\|_2$),
$$\operatorname{Re}(\mathbf{x}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}) \,
\operatorname{Re}(\mathbf{x}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}^{-1}\mathbf{x})
  \le \frac{(\kappa + 1)^2}{4\kappa} \|\mathbf{x}\|^4\,.$$
:::
