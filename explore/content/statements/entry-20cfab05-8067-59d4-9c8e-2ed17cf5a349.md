::: theorem result:gordon-inequality name="Gordon's min-max inequality"
for centred finite Gaussian families $(X_{ut})$,
$(Y_{ut})$ indexed by `U × T` whose increments are smaller for $X$ within a block,
$\mathbb{E}(X_{ut} - X_{us})^2 \le \mathbb{E}(Y_{ut} - Y_{us})^2$, and larger for $X$ across
blocks, $\mathbb{E}(Y_{ut} - Y_{u's})^2 \le \mathbb{E}(X_{ut} - X_{u's})^2$ for $u \ne u'$,
$$\mathbb{E}\min_u \max_t X_{ut} \le \mathbb{E}\min_u \max_t Y_{ut}\,.$$
The cited formulation assumes equal variances and gives a tail comparison; this is the expectation
form from increments alone, with the two families on different probability spaces.
:::
