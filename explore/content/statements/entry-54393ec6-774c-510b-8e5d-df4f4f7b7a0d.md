::: theorem result:cg-galerkin-condition name="The Galerkin condition"
For positive definite $\mathbf{A}$, the residual of the CG iterate is orthogonal to the Krylov space $\mathcal{K}_q(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{r}_0)$, where $\mathbf{r}_0 = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_0$:

$$\mathbf{K}_q(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{r}_0)^{\mathsf{H}}\bigl(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{A}\,\mathsf{cgIterate}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{x}_0, q)\bigr) = \mathbf{0}.$$

For real symmetric $\mathbf{A}$, the cited projection-method convention takes $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{K}$: CG is the orthogonal projection method onto $\mathcal{K}_q(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{r}_0)$, and this orthogonality condition defines the method.
:::
