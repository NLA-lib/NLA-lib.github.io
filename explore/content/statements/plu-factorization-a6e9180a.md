::: definition name="PLU factorization"
With the row-permutation convention used here, $\mathbf{P}\mathbf{A}=\mathbf{L}\mathbf{U}$, where $\mathbf{P}$ is a permutation matrix, $\mathbf{L}$ is unit lower triangular, and $\mathbf{U}$ is upper triangular.
:::
