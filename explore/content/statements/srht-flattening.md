::: theorem result:srht-flattening name="Flattening the leverage scores"
Let $\mathbf{F}$ be an $n\times n$ unitary transform satisfying $n|\mathbf{F}_{ij}|^2\le\beta$, and let $\mathbf{D}$ contain independent Rademacher signs. For an input subspace of dimension $d$ and $t\ge0$, with failure probability at most $ne^{-t}$, every leverage score after applying $\mathbf{F}\mathbf{D}$ is at most

$$\frac{\beta(d+2\sqrt{dt}+2t)}{n}.$$

Random signs and the bounded orthogonal transform spread the subspace across coordinates before row sampling.
:::
