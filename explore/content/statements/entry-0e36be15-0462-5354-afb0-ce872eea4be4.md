::: theorem result:gsvd-normalized-pair name="The common-kernel block of a GSVD"
For normalized generalized singular coordinates
of $\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B}$, their common kernel is precisely the span of the columns of
$\mathbf{X}$ with $c_i^2+s_i^2=0$. This includes an empty common-kernel block and the case
where every coordinate is inactive. This source states the block as an indexed span, without permuting the inactive coordinates to the end.
:::
