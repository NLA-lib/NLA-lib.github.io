::: theorem result:gauss-lobatto-quadrature name="Gauss–Lobatto quadrature"
For a finite measure $\mu$ supported in $[a, b]$, $a < b$, and
every $k$, the Lobatto rule with $k$ free nodes matches the first $2k + 2$ moments of $\mu$: it
integrates every real polynomial of degree at most $2k + 1$ exactly. The cited rule is for $\mu$ with more than $k$ interior support points; otherwise the rule is $\mu$ itself. The source locators require checking.
:::
