::: theorem result:szasz-inequality name="Normalized Szász inequality"
For a positive semidefinite matrix of dimension $d+1$,
the product of principal minors of order $j+1$, raised to $1/\binom{d}{j}$, decreases with
$j$ throughout $0\le j\le d$.
:::
