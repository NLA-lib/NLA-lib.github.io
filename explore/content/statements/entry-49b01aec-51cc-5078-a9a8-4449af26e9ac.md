::: theorem result:newton-schulz-sign-rounded-step name="One rounded Newton–Schulz step"
Under the arithmetic contract
`NLAlib.FloatMachine.IsArithAccurate` $u$ $\tau$ $\Omega$ with product constant $\mu$,
$\mu(n) \ge 0$, $u \ge 0$, $u(8 + \mu(n)) \le 1/10$, $0 \le \tau$, $2n\tau \le 1$ and
$\Omega \ge 9$, every stored $\mathbf{A}$ whose decoding (still written $\mathbf{A}$) is
Hermitian with $\|\mathbf{A}\|_2 \le 11/10$ satisfies
$$\Bigl\|\tfrac12\mathbf{A}(3\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{A}^2) - \tilde g(\mathbf{A})\Bigr\|_2 \le
4\sqrt n\,(8 + \mu(n))\,u \,,$$
$\tilde g$ is the decoded rounded step `NLAlib.newtonSchulzRounded` (constants $3$ and $4$ are computed by
the machine's conversion). The bound applies in the stated regime $\|\mathbf{A}\|_2\le11/10$.

Note: The cited Lemma 2.1 instead gives $\tfrac12(7 + (6 + \mu)a^2)a\,u$ for $\|\mathbf{A}\|_2 \le a$ without the Hermitian part. The factor $\sqrt n$ is the cost of storing the result exactly Hermitian.
:::
