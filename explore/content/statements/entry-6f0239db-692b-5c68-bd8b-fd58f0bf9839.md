::: theorem result:lanczos-reorth-orthogonality name="Orthogonality of the computed basis on the admissible prefix"
Under the fixed-point
condition $\sqrt{3(k+1)}\,\mu \le \eta_0 \le 1/100$, for every $m \le k + 1$,
$\|\mathbf{V}_m^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{V}_m - \mathbf{I}\|_{\mathsf F} \le \sqrt{3m}\,\mu$.
Paige treats Lanczos with reorthogonalization; Giraud, Langou, Rozloznik and Van den Eshof give
the CGS2 kernel constants.
:::
