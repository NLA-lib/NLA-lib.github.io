::: theorem result:countsketch-osi name="CountSketch OSI"
For $\varepsilon > 0$ and nonempty `k`, a CountSketch is an oblivious
subspace injection for dimension $d$ with constant $1 - \varepsilon$ and failure probability
$$(d^2 + d)/(\lvert k \rvert \varepsilon^2) \,:$$
the lower half of the OSE, with isotropy.
The stated notion is defined in the reference; its Table 3 lists CountSketch only
as an OSE, with $k = O(r^2)$.
:::
