::: theorem result:lanczos-dilated-recurrence name="The dilated recurrence"
Assuming $k\varepsilon \le 1/100$, the exchange rule gives the following bound on the dilated
residual: the orthonormal dilated vectors satisfy the computed recurrence with the computed coefficients up
to <!-- value lanczosDilatedResidualFactor k -->
$$\|\hat{\mathbf{E}}\|_{\mathsf{F}} =
\|\hat{\mathbf{H}}\hat{\mathbf{V}}_k - \hat{\mathbf{V}}_{k+1}\underline{\mathbf{T}}_k\|_{\mathsf{F}}
\le 280k^3\varepsilon\|\mathbf{H}\|_2 \,.$$


Note: No printed source; the maintainer's draft, `lem:exchange` and `lem:dilated-recurrence`, state $\lesssim_k \varepsilon\|\mathbf{A}\|_2$ (its formalization: $240000k^6$ in the spectral norm).
:::
