::: definition name="Residuals and spectral separation"
For a candidate eigenpair $(\lambda,\mathbf{x})$, the residual is $\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}-\lambda\mathbf{x}$. For a candidate diagonalization, a matrix residual is $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{U}-\mathbf{U}\boldsymbol{\Lambda}$. A small residual measures how well the equation is satisfied. Converting it to a distance from a particular invariant subspace requires the relevant spectral-separation assumptions.
:::
