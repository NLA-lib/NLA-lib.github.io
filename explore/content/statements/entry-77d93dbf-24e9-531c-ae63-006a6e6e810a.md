::: theorem result:orthogonal-polynomial-existence name="Existence of the orthogonal polynomials"
For a finite measure $\mu$ supported in
$[a, b]$ and every $k$, the monic polynomial $\pi_k$ of degree $k$ is orthogonal in $L^2(\mu)$
to every real polynomial of degree less than $k$. No nondegeneracy is needed: past the number of
support points, $\pi_k$ vanishes on the support. The cited source treats $k$ below the number of support points. Which of Theorems 1.6–1.8
states existence remains to be checked.
:::
