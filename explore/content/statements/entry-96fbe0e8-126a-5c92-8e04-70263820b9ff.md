::: theorem result:cgls name="CGLS"
Let $\mathbb{K}\in\{\mathbb{R},\mathbb{C}\}$, $\mathbf{A}\in\mathbb{K}^{m\times n}$ have full column rank, $\mathbf{b}\in\mathbb{K}^m$ be the right-hand side, and $\mathbf{x}_0\in\mathbb{K}^n$ be the initial guess. In exact arithmetic, define the CGLS iterate $\mathbf{x}_q=\mathsf{cgIterate}(\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{b}, \mathbf{x}_0, q)$ by applying [conjugate gradients](/explore/algorithms/conjugate-gradient/) to the normal equations. If $\mathbf{x}_\star$ solves $\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_\star=\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{b}$ and $\kappa=\kappa(\mathbf{A})$, then for every nonnegative integer $q$,

$$\|\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{x}_\star - \mathbf{x}_q)\|^2 \le
4\Bigl(\frac{\kappa - 1}{\kappa + 1}\Bigr)^{2q}\|\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{x}_\star - \mathbf{x}_0)\|^2
\,,$$

where the left and right sides are the quadratic forms induced by $\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}$.
:::
