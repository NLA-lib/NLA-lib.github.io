::: theorem result:moore-penrose-uniqueness name="Uniqueness of the pseudoinverse"
A matrix has at most one Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse:
if $\mathbf{B}$ and $\mathbf{C}$ both satisfy the four Penrose identities for $\mathbf{A}$, then
$\mathbf{B} = \mathbf{C}$.

:::
