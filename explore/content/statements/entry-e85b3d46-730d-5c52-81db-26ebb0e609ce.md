::: theorem result:multivariate-golden-thompson name="Multivariate Golden–Thompson"
For Hermitian matrices $\mathbf{H}_j$ and $1\le p<\infty$,
$$\log\Big\|\exp\Big(\sum_j\mathbf{H}_j\Big)\Big\|_{S_p}
  \le \int_{\mathbb R}\!\beta_0(t)\log\Big\|\prod_j\exp((1+it)\mathbf{H}_j)\Big\|_{S_p}\,dt,$$
where $\beta_0(t)=\pi/(2(\cosh(\pi t)+1))$ and multiplication follows the order of the list.

The formal proof adapts `tchen-research/NLAlib@77514ce`.
:::
