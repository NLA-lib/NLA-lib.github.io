::: definition name="Stochastic Lanczos quadrature"
For $s\ge1$ random probes, approximate each quadratic form $\mathbf{g}_i^{\mathsf{H}}f(\mathbf{A})\mathbf{g}_i$ by Lanczos quadrature and average the results. Isotropic probes connect their average to a spectral-function trace. Sampling error and quadrature error are distinct contributions; both must be controlled to obtain a trace-estimation guarantee.
:::
