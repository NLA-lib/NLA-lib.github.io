::: theorem result:gauss-radau-quadrature name="Gauss–Radau quadrature"
For a finite measure $\mu$ supported in $[a, b]$,
$c \in \{a, b\}$ and every $k$, the Radau rule with prescribed node $c$ and $k$ free nodes
matches the first $2k + 1$ moments of $\mu$: it integrates every real polynomial of degree at
most $2k$ exactly. The source locators require checking; the cited rule is for $\mu$ with more than $k$ support points; otherwise the rule is $\mu$ itself.
:::
