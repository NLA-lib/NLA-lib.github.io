::: definition decl:NLAlib.girardHutchinson name="Girard–Hutchinson estimator"
For $s\ge1$ probes $\mathbf{g}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{g}_s$, estimate the trace by

$$\widehat t=\frac1s\sum_{i=1}^s\mathbf{g}_i^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{g}_i.$$

The standard random estimator uses [isotropic probes](/explore/statements/cb5e0fa7-3425-5848-83bd-fd73b4aa314e/), such as independent Rademacher or standard Gaussian vectors. Variance and probability guarantees depend on the probe distribution and matrix assumptions.
:::
