::: theorem result:sphere-projection-beta name="The projection of a uniform unit vector is Beta"
For $\mathbf{u}$ uniform on the unit
sphere of $\mathbb{R}^n$ (law `uniformSphereVector n`) and $\mathbf{V}$ with $k$ orthonormal
columns, $0 < k < n$,
$$\|\mathbf{V}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{u}\|^2 \sim
\mathrm{Beta}\Big(\frac{k}{2}, \frac{n-k}{2}\Big)\,.$$

Note: No printed source.
:::
