::: theorem result:hutchinson-trace-estimator name="Hutchinson's identity"
For an isotropic probe $\mathbf{x}$, the quadratic form
$\mathbf{x}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}$ is an unbiased estimator of
$\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{A}$: $\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}]
= \operatorname{tr}\mathbf{A}$.
The referenced result is for the averaged Girard–Hutchinson estimator; this statement treats one probe.
:::
