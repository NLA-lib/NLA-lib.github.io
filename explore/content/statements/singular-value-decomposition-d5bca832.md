::: definition name="Singular-value decomposition"
A singular-value decomposition is $\mathbf{A}=\mathbf{U}\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\mathbf{V}^{\mathsf{H}}$, with unitary factors $\mathbf{U},\mathbf{V}$ and a rectangular diagonal matrix $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}$ containing nonnegative [singular values](/explore/statements/581c9098-24f3-55b6-bd36-a47af48b21ff/) in decreasing order. Retaining the leading $k$ singular components defines a rank-at-most-$k$ truncation. The decomposition and its truncation are mathematical objects; concrete SVD algorithms have separate execution and accuracy questions.
:::
