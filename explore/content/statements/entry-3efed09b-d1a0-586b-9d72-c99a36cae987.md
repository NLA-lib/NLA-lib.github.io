::: theorem result:karamata-inequality name="Karamata's inequality"
If $\mathbf{x}$ is [majorized](/explore/statements/94212462-4541-5bf1-96ea-38d5e791525e/) by $\mathbf{y}$ and $\varphi$ is
convex on $\mathbb{R}$, then $\sum_i \varphi(\mathbf{x}_i) \le \sum_i \varphi(\mathbf{y}_i)$.
:::
