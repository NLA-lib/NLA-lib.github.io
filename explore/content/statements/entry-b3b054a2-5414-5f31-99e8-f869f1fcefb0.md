::: theorem result:qr-existence name="Every matrix with at least as many rows as columns has a QR factorization"
If $\lvert n \rvert \le \lvert m \rvert$, then a QR factorization with nonnegative diagonal is given by
$\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{Q}\mathbf{R}$ with $\mathbf{R}_{i,i} \ge 0$.

:::
