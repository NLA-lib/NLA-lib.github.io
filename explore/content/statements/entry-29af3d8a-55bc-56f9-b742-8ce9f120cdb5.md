::: theorem result:least-squares-qr name="Least squares through QR"
When the diagonal of $\mathbf{R}$ has no zero (full column
rank), $\mathbf{x}$ minimises $\|\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{b}\|$ exactly when
$\mathbf{R}\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{Q}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{b}$.

:::
