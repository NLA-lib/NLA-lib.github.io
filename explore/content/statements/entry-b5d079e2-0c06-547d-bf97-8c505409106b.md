::: theorem result:cg-orthogonality name="CG: orthogonal residuals and conjugate directions"
For positive definite $\mathbf{A}$, the CG search directions satisfy

$$\mathbf{p}_j^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{p}_k = 0 \qquad (j < k).$$

For real symmetric $\mathbf{A}$, this is relation (5:3b).
:::
