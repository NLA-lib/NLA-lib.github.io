::: definition decl:NLAlib.oddMatrixIterate name="Polynomial polar iteration"
Choose a sequence of odd polynomial updates, evaluated in Gram form as $\mathbf{X}\,p_j(\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{X})$. This applies the corresponding odd scalar polynomial to the [singular values](/explore/statements/581c9098-24f3-55b6-bd36-a47af48b21ff/) while retaining their singular-vector directions. Coefficient choices and the initial singular-value interval determine the certified approximation to the polar factor.
:::
