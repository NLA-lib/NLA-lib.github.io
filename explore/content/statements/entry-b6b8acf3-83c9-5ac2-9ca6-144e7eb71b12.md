::: theorem result:total-least-squares-svd name="Total least squares infimum"
For augmented data $\mathbf{C}$, the infimum of the
squared Frobenius norms of feasible corrections is the least eigenvalue of its full domain
Gram matrix. This allows nonattainment and repeated minimum eigenvalues. The statement also covers wide and empty-row data.
:::
