::: theorem result:sampling-without-replacement-ose name="Row sampling without replacement"
If the leverage scores of $\mathbf{U}$ (any shape and
rank) satisfy $\ell_i(\mathbf{U}) \le L / \lvert n \rvert$, the row-sampling sketch without
replacement with $k$ samples is a [subspace embedding](/explore/statements/dd0c654f-b783-516b-b7c3-04b96f1a90f3/) for $\operatorname{range}\mathbf{U}$ with
constants $1 \mp \varepsilon$ outside probability
$$2 \operatorname{rank}\mathbf{U} \cdot e^{-\varepsilon^2 k / (3L)} \,,$$
for $0 \le \varepsilon \le 1$. Sampling without replacement is never worse than with replacement
(`NLAlib.hasSubspaceEmbedding_of_hasLaw_samplingSketch` at uniform weights has the same bound).
The cited SRHT sampling result assumes orthonormal columns and uses $L = \lvert n \rvert \max_i \ell_i$,
with both Chernoff tails retained before weakening. The cited i.i.d. form gives the
with-replacement bound.
:::
