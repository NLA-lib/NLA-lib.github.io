::: definition name="Early and late truncation"
Choose an intermediate rank $s\ge k$. First retain $[\mathbf{A}\boldsymbol{\Omega}]_s$, then form $\mathbf{B}_s=\mathbf{P}_{[\mathbf{A}\boldsymbol{\Omega}]_s}\mathbf{A}$ and return $[\mathbf{B}_s]_k$. The intermediate rank $s$ and output rank $k$ are separate parameters.
:::
