::: theorem result:oblique-projector-canonical-form name="Unitary canonical form of an idempotent"
For an idempotent matrix, in an orthonormal basis of its range and its
orthogonal complement, the diagonal blocks are the identity and zero. The rectangular upper-right
block is diagonal with entries $\sqrt{\sigma_i(\mathbf{P})^2-1}$.

:::
