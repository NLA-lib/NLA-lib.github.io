::: theorem result:subgaussian-ose name="Sub-Gaussian OSE"
For a random $\mathbf{X}$ with independent, isotropic, sub-Gaussian rows
with proxy $c$, $\mathbf{S} = \lvert k \rvert^{-1/2} \, \mathbf{X}$ is an [oblivious subspace embedding](/explore/statements/dd0c654f-b783-516b-b7c3-04b96f1a90f3/) for dimension $d$ with constants $1 \mp \varepsilon$ and failure
probability <!-- value subgaussianJLConstant -->
$$2 \cdot 21^d \, e^{-\frac{1}{36} \lvert k \rvert
\min(16\varepsilon^2/(25c^2), \, 4\varepsilon/(5c))} \,,$$
the constant being `subgaussianJLConstant`: JL plus a net of radius $1/10$ at $4\varepsilon/5$
(`NLAlib.HasJLProperty.isObliviousSubspaceEmbedding`). Vershynin's singular-value estimate uses $K^2 \max(\delta, \delta^2)$ with an unspecified
constant; it dominates the row theorem's Gram sandwich in every regime.
:::
