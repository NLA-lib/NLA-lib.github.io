::: theorem result:eigenvalue-order-and-counting name="Strict subspace test from above"
If the Rayleigh quotient of a Hermitian matrix is
strictly below $c$ on the nonzero vectors of a subspace of codimension at most $k$, then
$\lambda_k(\mathbf{A})<c$ for $k$ in range.
:::
