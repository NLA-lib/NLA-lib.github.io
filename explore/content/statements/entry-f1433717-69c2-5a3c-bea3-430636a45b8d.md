::: theorem result:orthogonal-polynomial-recurrence name="The three-term recurrence"
For every measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and every $k$,
$$\pi_{k+2} = (x - \alpha_{k+1})\pi_{k+1} - \beta_{k+1}\pi_k\,,$$
with $\alpha_k = \int x\pi_k^2\,d\mu/\int\pi_k^2\,d\mu$ and
$\beta_k = \int\pi_k^2\,d\mu/\int\pi_{k-1}^2\,d\mu$ (zero when the denominator is).

Note: The cited sources state this as a theorem about orthogonal polynomials; this entry gives the relation
as a definition.
:::
