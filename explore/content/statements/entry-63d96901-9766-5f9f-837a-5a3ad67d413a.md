::: theorem result:newton-schulz-sign-perturbed-defect name="Quadratic decrease of the defect under perturbation"
Lemma 2.4 (first part): if
$20\lvert \xi \rvert \le \lvert x \rvert \le 1 - \sqrt{10\lvert \xi \rvert}$, then
$$\lvert 1 - (g(x) + \xi)^2 \rvert \le \lvert 1 - x^2 \rvert^2 \,.$$
