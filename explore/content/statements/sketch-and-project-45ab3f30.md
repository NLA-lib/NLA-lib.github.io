::: definition name="Sketch-and-project"
For a sequence of sketches $\mathbf{S}_j$, set $\mathbf{x}_{j+1}=\mathbf{x}_j+(\mathbf{S}_j\mathbf{A})^\dagger\mathbf{S}_j(\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_j)$. This is a minimum-norm correction for the sketched residual. Convergence results specify consistency, the starting error, independence, and a lower bound on the mean projected row-space operator.
:::
