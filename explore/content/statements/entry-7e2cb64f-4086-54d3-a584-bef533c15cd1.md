::: theorem result:operator-capacity-near-balanced-certificate name="Capacity-to-balance converse"
For a trace-preserving Kraus family with
$e^{-\delta}\le\operatorname{cap}(T)$ and $\delta\le1/6$, its two marginals satisfy
$$\|T(I)-I\|_{\mathsf{F}}^2+\|T^{\mathsf{H}}(I)-I\|_{\mathsf{F}}^2\le6\delta.$$

:::
