::: theorem result:nuclear-spectral-duality name="The nuclear norm is the dual of the spectral norm"
The nuclear norm is the largest Frobenius inner
product with a matrix of spectral norm at most one,
$$\|\mathbf{A}\|_* = \max_{\|\mathbf{B}\|_2 \le 1}
  \langle \mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B} \rangle_{\mathsf{F}}\,.$$

:::
