::: theorem result:ordered-schatten-trace-deficit name="Nuclear norm of a positive semidefinite difference"
For $\mathbf{A} \succeq \mathbf{B} \succeq \mathbf{0}$, the nuclear norm of the residual is exactly
$\|\mathbf{A}\|_* - \|\mathbf{B}\|_*$.

:::
