::: theorem result:laplacian-resistance-leverage name="Edge resistance and leverage"
For a nonnegatively weighted edge, its weight times
its effective resistance equals the leverage score of its row of the weighted incidence
matrix. The identity also holds for disconnected graphs.
:::
