::: theorem result:lanczos-fa-recurrence name="The Lanczos recurrence computes Lanczos-FA"
for Hermitian $\mathbf{A}$ and every $q$:
$\mathsf{lanczosFA}(\mathbf{A}, f, \mathbf{b}, q + 1) =
\|\mathbf{b}\|\,\mathbf{Q}f(\mathbf{T})\mathbf{e}_0$ with $\mathbf{Q}$ and $\mathbf{T}$ from
$q + 1$ steps of the recurrence. .
:::
