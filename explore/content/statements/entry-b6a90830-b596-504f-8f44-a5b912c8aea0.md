::: theorem result:gaussian-log-sobolev name="Gross's logarithmic Sobolev inequality"
For the standard Gaussian on a Euclidean space:
$\operatorname{Ent}(g^2) \le 2\, \mathbb{E}\|\nabla g\|^2$ for $g$ of class $C^1$ with $g^2$ and
$\|\nabla g\|^2$ integrable.
:::
