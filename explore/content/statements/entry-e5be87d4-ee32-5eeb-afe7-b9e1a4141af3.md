::: theorem result:haar-frame-uniqueness name="Characterization of the Haar frame"
a random matrix with almost surely orthonormal
columns whose law is a left unitarily invariant probability measure is a Haar frame. No
$\lvert k \rvert \le \lvert n \rvert$ is needed: without it there are no frames, and `hQ` cannot
hold for a
probability law. This is the uniqueness of the invariant measure on the Stiefel manifold; here it is proved
against `haarFrame n n` directly.
:::
