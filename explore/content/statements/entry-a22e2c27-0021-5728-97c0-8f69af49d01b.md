::: theorem result:hardy-littlewood-polya name="Hardy–Littlewood–Pólya"
$\mathbf{x}$ is majorized by $\mathbf{y}$ exactly when
$\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{D}\mathbf{y}$ for some doubly stochastic matrix $\mathbf{D}$.

:::
