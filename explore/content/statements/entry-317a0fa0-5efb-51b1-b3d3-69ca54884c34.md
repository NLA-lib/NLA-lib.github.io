::: theorem result:least-squares-pseudoinverse name="The pseudoinverse solves least squares"
For a real or complex scalar field $\Bbbk$, a matrix
$\mathbf{A}\in\Bbbk^{m\times n}$ and right-hand side $\mathbf{b}\in\Bbbk^m$,
the pseudoinverse product $\mathbf{A}^{+}\mathbf{b}$ is a
least-squares solution to $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}\approx\mathbf{b}$.

:::
