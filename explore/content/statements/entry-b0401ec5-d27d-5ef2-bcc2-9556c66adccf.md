::: theorem result:newton-schulz-sign-perturbed-defect-additive name="The defect bound with an additive error"
Lemma 2.4 (second part): if
$\lvert x \rvert \le \sqrt2$ and $\lvert \xi \rvert \le 1$, then
$$\lvert 1 - (g(x) + \xi)^2 \rvert \le \lvert 1 - x^2 \rvert^2 + 4\lvert \xi \rvert \,.$$
:::
