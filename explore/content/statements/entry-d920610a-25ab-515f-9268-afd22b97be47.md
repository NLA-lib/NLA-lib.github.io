::: theorem result:matrix-exponential-frechet-integral name="Fréchet derivative of the exponential"
For $n\times n$ real or complex matrices $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{E}$, the Fréchet derivative of the matrix exponential in direction $\mathbf{E}$ is

$$\mathrm{D}\exp(\mathbf{A})[\mathbf{E}]=\int_0^1 \exp((1-s)\mathbf{A})\,\mathbf{E}\,\exp(s\mathbf{A})\,ds.$$

:::
