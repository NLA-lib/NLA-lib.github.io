::: theorem result:jl-rademacher name="Sign-sketch distributional JL"
Let $k,n$ be finite index sets, and let $\mathbf{X}$ be a random $|k|\times|n|$ sign matrix with independent Rademacher entries. If $k$ is nonempty, set $\mathbf{S}=|k|^{-1/2}\mathbf{X}$; if $k$ is empty, let $\mathbf{S}$ be the empty zero-row matrix. For every unit vector $\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}$ and every $\varepsilon\ge0$,
$$\mathbb{P}\bigl[\varepsilon \le \lvert \|\mathbf{S}\mathbf{x}\|^2 - 1 \rvert\bigr]
\le 2 e^{-\lvert k \rvert (\varepsilon^2/4 - \varepsilon^3/6)} \,,$$

Note: The proof derives this from `NLAlib.hasJLProperty_of_isSubgaussianVector_of_integral_pow_four_le` and the identity $\mathbb{E}\langle\boldsymbol{\xi},\mathbf{x}\rangle^4=\operatorname{Var}(\langle\boldsymbol{\xi},\mathbf{x}\rangle^2)+\|\mathbf{x}\|^4=3\|\mathbf{x}\|^4-2\sum_j\mathbf{x}_j^4$ (`NLAlib.variance_quadForm_of_hasLaw_rademacherVector`). Woodruff discusses the bound; the constants are attributed to Achlioptas, but the inherited locator remains to be confirmed. For $\varepsilon\gtrsim1.43$, `NLAlib.hasJLProperty_of_isSubgaussianVector` at $c=1$ gives a better bound.
:::
