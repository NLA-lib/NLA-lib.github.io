::: definition name="Loewner order and majorization"
For [Hermitian matrices](/explore/statements/49f5c3f6-f926-5bc6-b401-16993ec1e357/), $\mathbf{A}\preceq\mathbf{B}$ means $\mathbf{x}^{\mathsf{H}}(\mathbf{B}-\mathbf{A})\mathbf{x}\ge0$ for every vector $\mathbf{x}$. Majorization instead compares partial sums of ordered scalar lists, such as eigenvalues or [singular values](/explore/statements/581c9098-24f3-55b6-bd36-a47af48b21ff/). A statement about one of these orders does not automatically imply a statement about every other matrix quantity.
:::
