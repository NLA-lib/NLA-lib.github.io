::: theorem result:multivariate-araki-lieb-thirring name="Multivariate Araki–Lieb–Thirring"
For positive semidefinite factors $\mathbf{A}_j$, $p \ge 1$
and $0 < r < 1$, with both products in the given order,
$$
\log \left\|\left\lvert\prod_j \mathbf{A}_j^r\right\rvert^{1/r}\right\|_{S_p}
\le \int_{\mathbb{R}} \beta_r(t) \log \left\|\prod_j \mathbf{A}_j^{1+it}\right\|_{S_p}\,dt.
$$
Here $\beta_r$ is the strip Poisson density and the extended logarithm takes the value
$-\infty$ at zero.
The formal proof adapts `tchen-research/NLAlib@77514ce`.
:::
