::: theorem result:generalized-nystrom-tail name="Generalized Nyström to relative error"
Let $m,n,\ell,s$ be finite dimensions and $k,d\in\mathbb{N}$. Let $\mathbf{A}\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$ be fixed, and let $\mathbf{\Omega}\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times\ell}$ and $\mathbf{\Psi}\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times s}$ be independent random matrices. If
$\mathbf{\Omega}^{\mathsf{H}}$ injects
$\operatorname{range}\llbracket\mathbf{A}\rrbracket_k^{\mathsf{H}}$ with constant $a > 0$ except
with probability $\delta$ and has the $(\varepsilon, \delta_1, q)$ JL moment property, and an
independent real $\mathbf{\Psi}^{\mathsf{H}}$ is an OSI for dimension
$d \ge \min(\operatorname{rank}\mathbf{A}, \ell)$ (constant $a' > 0$, failure probability $\delta'$)
with the $(\varepsilon', \delta_2, q')$ JL moment property, with $\varepsilon, \varepsilon' > 0$,
$q, q' \ge 2$ and $\delta_1, \delta_2 \ge 0$, then
$$\|\mathbf{A} - \mathsf{gn}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{\Omega}, \mathbf{\Psi})\|_{\mathsf{F}}^2 \le
\Bigl(1 + \frac{d\,\varepsilon'^2}{a'^2}\Bigr)\Bigl(1 + \frac{k\varepsilon^2}{a^2}\Bigr)
\|\mathbf{A} - \llbracket\mathbf{A}\rrbracket_k\|_{\mathsf{F}}^2$$
except with probability $\delta + \delta_1 + \delta' + \delta_2$, where $\mathsf{gn}$ denotes the [generalized Nyström approximation](/explore/statements/a93c5a2a-f071-5e53-ad6e-926ccafcbdad/).

Note: The constant-factor neighbour is `NLAlib.HasSubspaceInjection.measureReal_frobNorm_sub_generalizedNystrom_sq_gt_le`. The cited theorem uses $\mathbf{R}$ as a $(1 + O(\varepsilon))$ embedding of the row space of $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{A}$ and leaves the multiplication step implicit; this statement makes it explicit.
:::
