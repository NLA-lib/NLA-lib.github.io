::: theorem result:lanczos-breakdown name="Lanczos breaks down exactly at the grade"
For Hermitian $\mathbf{A}$ and the recurrence
from $\mathbf{b}$, $\beta_{j+1} = 0$ iff $\nu \le j + 1$, $\nu$ the grade of $\mathbf{b}$.
The cited Arnoldi breakdown criterion specializes to the symmetric Lanczos case at the first
zero coefficient; here the coefficient stays zero from the grade on.
:::
