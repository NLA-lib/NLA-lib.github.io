::: theorem result:amm-sampling-spectral name="Approximate matrix multiplication in the spectral norm by row sampling"
Let $k,n,q,r$ be finite index sets, with $k$ and $r$ nonempty, and let $\Bbbk$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For $\mathbf{A}\in\Bbbk^{n\times q}$ and $\mathbf{B}\in\Bbbk^{n\times r}$, take weights $p_i\ge0$ summing to $1$, with $\|\mathbf{A}_i\|^2 \le a p_i$ and $\|\mathbf{B}_i\|^2 \le b p_i$ for every row $i$, where $a,b\ge0$. Each row of the $|k|\times|n|$ row-sampling sketch $\mathbf{S}$ is $(|k|p_i)^{-1/2}\mathbf{e}_i^{\mathsf{H}}$ when its independently sampled index $i$ has $p_i>0$; it is zero for $p_i=0$. The indices are sampled with probabilities $p_i$. For $t\ge0$, with probability taken over this sketch,
$$\mathbb{P}\bigl[t \le \|\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{S}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{B} -
\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{B}\|_2\bigr] \le (\lvert q\rvert + \lvert r\rvert)
\exp\Bigl(-\frac{\lvert k\rvert t^2/2}{\max(b\|\mathbf{A}\|_2^2, a\|\mathbf{B}\|_2^2) +
2\sqrt{ab}\,t/3}\Bigr)\,.$$

Note: This follows by applying rectangular matrix Bernstein (`NLAlib.measureReal_specNorm_sum_ge_le_bernstein`) to the centered atoms. Choosing $p_i\propto\|\mathbf{A}_i\|^2+\|\mathbf{B}_i\|^2$ yields the stable-rank form. The source marks the locator of the Tropp corollary for checking.
:::
