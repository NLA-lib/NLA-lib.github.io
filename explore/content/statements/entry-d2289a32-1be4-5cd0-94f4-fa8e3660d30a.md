::: theorem result:hermite-interpolation-remainder name="The Hermite interpolation remainder"
Let $a < b$, $m$ a nonempty multiset of points of
$[a, b]$ with every point of multiplicity at least $2$ in $(a, b)$, $f$ continuous on $[a, b]$
and $C^{\lvert m\rvert}$ on $(a, b)$, and $H$ a real polynomial of degree less than
$\lvert m\rvert$ with $H^{(k)}(y) = f^{(k)}(y)$ for every $y \in m$ and every $k$ below the
multiplicity of $y$. Then for every $x \in [a, b]$ there is $\xi \in (a, b)$ with
$$f(x) - H(x) = \frac{f^{(\lvert m\rvert)}(\xi)}{\lvert m\rvert!}\prod_{y\in m}(x - y)\,.$$
The source locators require checking; the cited results treat distinct nodes with multiplicity at
most $2$ and place $\xi$ in the hull of the nodes and $x$. This statement allows any
multiplicities and simple nodes at the endpoints.
:::
