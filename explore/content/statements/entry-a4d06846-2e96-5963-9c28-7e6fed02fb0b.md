::: theorem result:schatten-even-moment-smoothness name="Uniform smoothness for even Schatten moments"
For real or complex rectangular matrices
$\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B}$ of the same shape and a positive even integer $p=2r$,
$$\left(\frac{\|\mathbf{A}+\mathbf{B}\|_{S_p}^p+\|\mathbf{A}-\mathbf{B}\|_{S_p}^p}{2}
\right)^{2/p} \le \|\mathbf{A}\|_{S_p}^2+(p-1)\|\mathbf{B}\|_{S_p}^2.$$
The cited smoothness result is stated for even exponents.
:::
