::: theorem result:cholesky-existence name="Existence of Cholesky factors"
A square matrix admits a [Cholesky factor](/explore/statements/d00946c1-7d12-50e4-a6da-635456a9fca7/) with positive real diagonal exactly when it is positive definite. This characterizes existence of the mathematical factorization; a rounded implementation needs a separate execution and accuracy result.
:::
