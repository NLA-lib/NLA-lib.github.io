::: theorem result:generalized-schur-pencil-determinant name="Homogeneous determinant in QZ form"
Suppose unitary $\mathbf{Q},\mathbf{Z}$ reduce
$\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B}$ to upper triangular $\mathbf{S},\mathbf{T}$. For all $\alpha,\beta$,
$\det(\beta\mathbf{A}-\alpha\mathbf{B})$ equals
$\det(\mathbf{Q})\det(\mathbf{Z}^{\mathsf{H}})\prod_i(\beta s_{ii}-\alpha t_{ii})$.
Derived from the cited triangularization, including singular pencils.
:::
