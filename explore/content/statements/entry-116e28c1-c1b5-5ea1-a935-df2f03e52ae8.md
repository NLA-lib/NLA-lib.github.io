::: theorem result:newton-schulz-sign-perturbed-step-modulus name="The perturbed Newton–Schulz step does not decrease the modulus"
Lemma 2.3 (second part) states:
if $\lvert \xi \rvert \le u$ and $\tfrac83 u \le \lvert x \rvert \le 1 - \tfrac83 u$, then
$\lvert x \rvert \le \lvert g(x) + \xi \rvert$..
:::
