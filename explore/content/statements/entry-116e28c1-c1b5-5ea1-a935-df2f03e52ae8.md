::: theorem result:newton-schulz-sign-perturbed-step-modulus name="The perturbed Newton–Schulz step does not decrease the modulus"
For the scalar [Newton–Schulz sign iteration](/explore/algorithms/newton-schulz-sign/), let $g(x)=\tfrac12x(3-x^2)$. If $\lvert \xi \rvert \le u$ and $\tfrac83 u \le \lvert x \rvert \le 1 - \tfrac83 u$, then $\lvert x \rvert \le \lvert g(x) + \xi \rvert$.
:::
