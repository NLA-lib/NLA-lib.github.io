::: theorem result:subgaussian-maximal-inequality name="The maximal inequality"
the mean of the maximum of $N$ sub-Gaussian variables with common
proxy $c$ is at most $\sqrt{2 c \log N}$, under a probability measure. No independence is assumed.
:::
