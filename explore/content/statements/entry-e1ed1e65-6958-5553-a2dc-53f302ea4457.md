::: theorem result:chebyshev-extremal name="Extremal growth of Chebyshev polynomials"
If $\deg p\le q$ and $\lvert p\rvert\le1$ on
$[-1,1]$, then $\lvert p(x)\rvert\le\lvert T_q(x)\rvert$ for $\lvert x\rvert\ge1$. This is an
external-comparison corollary; the proof uses Mathlib's extremal
derivative inequality at order zero.
:::
