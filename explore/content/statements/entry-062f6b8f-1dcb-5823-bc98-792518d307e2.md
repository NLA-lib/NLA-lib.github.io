::: theorem result:polynomial-two-interval-inapproximability name="Polynomial inapproximability on two intervals"
For $\kappa \ge 2$ and every $q$, every
real polynomial $p$ of degree at most $q$ with $p(0) = 0$ has
$\max_{\lambda \in [-\kappa, -1] \cup [1, \kappa]} \lvert 1 - p(\lambda) \rvert \ge e^{-2q/\kappa}$:
$$e^{-2q/\kappa} \le \mathsf{residualMinimax}\bigl([-\kappa, -1] \cup [1, \kappa], q\bigr) \,.$$

Note: The cited Lemma 1 denotes this quantity by $\rho_d(\kappa)$ and defines it as the infimum over
$p(0) = 0$ of the maximum of $\lvert 1 - p \rvert$, which is `NLAlib.residualMinimax` of the
residual $1 - p$.
:::
