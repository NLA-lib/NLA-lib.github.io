::: theorem result:matrix-freedman name="Freedman's inequality for matrix martingales"
For $n$ nonempty, increments $\mathbf{X}_j$,
$\mathcal{F}_{j+1}$-measurable, square-integrable, conditionally centred
($\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{X}_j \mid \mathcal{F}_j] = \mathbf{0}$) and with
$\mathbf{X}_j \preceq L\mathbf{I}$ almost surely, $L > 0$ and $t \ge 0$,
$$\mathbb{P}\Bigl[\exists k,\ \lambda_0\Bigl(\sum_{j<k} \mathbf{X}_j\Bigr) \ge t \text{ and }
\Bigl\|\sum_{j<k} \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{X}_j^2 \mid \mathcal{F}_j]\Bigr\|_2 \le v\Bigr]
\le \lvert n\rvert \exp\Bigl(\frac{-t^2/2}{v + L t / 3}\Bigr)\,.$$
Stated in the cited theorem for Hermitian martingales over $\mathbb{C}$ with the conditional
variance as the predictable quadratic variation, here over any `RCLike` field, any finite index
type, with square-integrable increments. The constant is the same as in the cited theorem. There
is no hypothesis on $v$: for $v < 0$ the event is empty.
:::
