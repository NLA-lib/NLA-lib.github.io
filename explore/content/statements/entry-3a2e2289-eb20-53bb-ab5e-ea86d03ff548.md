::: theorem result:kkt-reduced-hessian name="Unique equality-constrained quadratic minima"
Let $\mathbf{H}$ be Hermitian and let
$\mathbf{B}$ have full row rank. For any basis $\mathbf{Z}$ of its kernel, every linear term
and every constraint right-hand side admit exactly one feasible minimizer if and only if
$\mathbf{Z}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{H}\mathbf{Z}$ is positive definite. For a square invertible
constraint matrix the feasible set is a singleton and the reduced matrix has size zero. The converse follows by considering the
homogeneous problem.
:::
