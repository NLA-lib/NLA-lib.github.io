::: definition name="Singular values"
The singular values of $\mathbf{A}$ are the nonnegative square roots of the eigenvalues of $\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}$, listed in decreasing order and with the appropriate zero padding. Equivalently, they are the diagonal values in a [singular-value decomposition](/explore/statements/d5bca832-63a1-5043-ab6e-03b8402a2a55/). A positive singular value represents an active direction; small singular values identify directions sensitive to perturbation or truncation.
:::
