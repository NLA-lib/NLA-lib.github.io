::: theorem result:ky-fan-eigenvalue-sums name="Ky Fan's majorization for sums"
The eigenvalues of $\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}$ are
[majorized](/explore/statements/94212462-4541-5bf1-96ea-38d5e791525e/) by the sums of the sorted eigenvalues of $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$: for Hermitian
$\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$, the vector
$(\lambda_i(\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}))_{i < \lvert n \rvert}$ is majorized by
$(\lambda_i(\mathbf{A}) + \lambda_i(\mathbf{B}))_{i < \lvert n \rvert}$.
:::
