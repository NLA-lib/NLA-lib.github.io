::: definition name="Late-truncated RSVD"
Form $\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{P}_{\mathbf{A}\mathbf\Omega}\mathbf{A}$, then return $[\mathbf{B}]_k$, a best rank-at-most-$k$ approximation to $\mathbf{B}$. Here $[\cdot]_k$ denotes truncated SVD.
:::
