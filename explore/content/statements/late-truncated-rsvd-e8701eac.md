::: definition name="Late-truncated RSVD"
Form $\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{P}_{\mathbf{A}\boldsymbol{\Omega}}\mathbf{A}$, then return $[\mathbf{B}]_k$, a best rank-at-most-$k$ approximation to $\mathbf{B}$. Here $[\cdot]_k$ denotes [truncated SVD](/explore/statements/22476dc5-4a8c-59f3-95a5-929740cd4e5b/).
:::
