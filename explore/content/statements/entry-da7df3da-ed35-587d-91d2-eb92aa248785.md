::: theorem result:gaussian-lipschitz-concentration name="Gaussian concentration of Lipschitz functions"
For $\mathbf{g}$ with law `stdGaussian E` and $f$ $L$-Lipschitz,
$f(\mathbf{g}) - \mathbb{E} f(\mathbf{g})$ is sub-Gaussian with proxy $L^2$.
:::
