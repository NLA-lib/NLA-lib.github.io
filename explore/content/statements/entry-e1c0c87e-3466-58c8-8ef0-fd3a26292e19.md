::: theorem result:horn-product-inequality name="Horn's product inequality"
For every $k$,
$$\prod_{i < k} \sigma_i(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B})
  \le \prod_{i < k} \sigma_i(\mathbf{A}) \, \sigma_i(\mathbf{B}) \,,$$
singular values indexed from $0$ and non-increasing.

:::
