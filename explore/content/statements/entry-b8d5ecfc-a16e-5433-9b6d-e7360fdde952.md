::: theorem result:matrix-monotone-loewner-criterion name="Löwner criterion"
On an open convex real set a differentiable function is
operator monotone in every finite size precisely when all its Löwner matrices are positive.
Repeated nodes and unbounded intervals are permitted.
:::
