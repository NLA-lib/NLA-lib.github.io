::: theorem result:minres-recurrence name="The Paige–Saunders iterates are the preconditioned MINRES iterates"
For invertible
Hermitian $\mathbf{A}$, positive definite $\mathbf{M}$ and every $k$, with no breakdown
hypothesis, $\mathbf{x}_k = \mathsf{pminresIterate}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{M}, \mathbf{b},
\mathbf{x}_0, k)$. Paige and Saunders treat the unpreconditioned case ($\mathbf{M} =
\mathbf{I}$) over $\mathbb{R}$ and
up to termination: iterate (6.9) of their recurrence solves the minimum-residual problem (2.7) in
the Lanczos basis.
:::
