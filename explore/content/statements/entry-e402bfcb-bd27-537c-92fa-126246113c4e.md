::: theorem result:best-approximant-interpolates name="The best approximation interpolates"
for $a < b$, $f$ continuous on $[a, b]$ and $p$ a
[best approximation of degree at most $n$](/explore/statements/89ab2263-0730-583c-89db-9695bea9604d/), there are $n + 1$ distinct points of $(a, b)$ at which
$p = f$; $p$ is the Lagrange interpolant of $f$ there (`Lagrange.eq_interpolate_of_eval_eq`).
The source note marks the Ch. 10 locator for checking; its formulation is on $[-1, 1]$.
:::
