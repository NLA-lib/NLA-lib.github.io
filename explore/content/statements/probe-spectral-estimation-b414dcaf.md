::: definition name="Probe spectral estimation"
For a [Hermitian](/explore/statements/49f5c3f6-f926-5bc6-b401-16993ec1e357/) $n\times n$ matrix and $s$ probes, average their spectral measures with normalization $1/(ns)$. With [isotropic probes](/explore/statements/cb5e0fa7-3425-5848-83bd-fd73b4aa314e/), the expected measure equals the empirical eigenvalue measure. [Lanczos quadrature](/explore/statements/6d8b12f4-2783-5f4b-8c76-53d3c62f914f/) provides finite-step approximations to the probe measures; its error must be accounted for separately.
:::
