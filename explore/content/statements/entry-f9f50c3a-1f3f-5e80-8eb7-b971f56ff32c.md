::: theorem result:gamma-ratio-beta name="Beta as a ratio of Gammas"
For $a, b, r > 0$, the image of
$\mathrm{Gamma}(a, r) \otimes \mathrm{Gamma}(b, r)$ (shapes $a$, $b$, common rate $r$) under
$$(x, y) \mapsto \frac{x}{x+y}$$
is $\mathrm{Beta}(a, b)$.

Note: No printed source.
:::
