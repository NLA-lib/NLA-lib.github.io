::: theorem decl:NLAlib.RefinementRun.norm_sub_x_succ_le name="One step" id=one-step
For $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_\star=\mathbf{b}$ and every $j<k$, with $\varphi$ and $\xi$ as above and no condition on their size,

$$\|\mathbf{x}_\star-\mathbf{x}_{j+1}\|\le\varphi\|\mathbf{x}_\star-\mathbf{x}_j\|+\xi\|\mathbf{x}_\star\|.$$
:::
