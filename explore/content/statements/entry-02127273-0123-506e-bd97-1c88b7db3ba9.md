::: theorem result:srht-osi name="The SRHT OSI"
For a bounded orthonormal $\mathbf{F}$ with constant $\beta$, nonempty `k`,
$\lvert k \rvert \le \lvert n \rvert$, $\varepsilon \ge 0$ and $t \ge 0$, the SRHT is an
oblivious subspace injection for dimension $d$ with constant $1 - \varepsilon$ and failure
probability
$$d \exp\Bigl(-\frac{\varepsilon^2 \lvert k \rvert}{2 \beta (d + 2\sqrt{d t} + 2t)}\Bigr) +
  \lvert n \rvert e^{-t}\,:$$
the lower matrix Chernoff half after flattening.
Camano, Epperly, Meyer, and Tropp discuss the OSI perspective and cite Tropp for the necessary
condition $k = \Omega(d \log d)$. Tropp’s Walsh–Hadamard result for orthonormal columns gives
$\sigma_{\min} \ge 1/\sqrt{6}$ at $4(\sqrt{d} + \sqrt{8 \log(d \lvert n \rvert)})^2 \log d$
rows, outside probability $3/d$ for both halves. Flattening
(`NLAlib.measureReal_exists_leverageScore_mul_diagonal_gt_le_of_hasLaw_rademacherVector`), then
`NLAlib.hasSubspaceInjection_of_hasLaw_samplingSketchWithoutReplacement` through
`NLAlib.hasSubspaceInjection_mul_of_indepFun_of_leverageScore_le`.
