::: theorem result:rouche-capelli name="Rouché–Capelli"
Adjoining the column $\mathbf{b}$ to $\mathbf{A}$ leaves the rank
unchanged exactly when $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ has a solution.
:::
