::: theorem result:arnoldi-recurrence name="The Arnoldi process computes the Hessenberg matrix"
For a square matrix $\mathbf{A}$ over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, a starting vector
$\mathbf{b}$, and $q\in\mathbb{N}$, let
$\mathbf{Q}_j=\mathsf{arnoldiMatrix}(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{b},j)$ be the canonical Arnoldi basis
after $j$ steps. The Hessenberg matrix computed by $q$ [Arnoldi steps](/explore/algorithms/arnoldi/)
is

$$\underline{\mathbf{H}}_q=\mathbf{Q}_{q+1}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q}_q.$$
:::
