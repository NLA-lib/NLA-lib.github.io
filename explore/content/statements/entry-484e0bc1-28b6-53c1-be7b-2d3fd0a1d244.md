::: theorem result:arnoldi-recurrence name="The Arnoldi process computes the Hessenberg matrix"
For every $\mathbf{A}$ and $q$,
the matrix computed by $q$ steps from $\mathbf{b}$ is $\underline{\mathbf{H}}_q =
\mathbf{Q}_{q+1}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q}_q$ for the canonical bases
$\mathbf{Q}_j = \mathsf{arnoldiMatrix}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{b}, j)$.
:::
