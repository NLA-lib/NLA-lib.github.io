::: theorem result:minimum-norm-least-squares name="The pseudoinverse solution has the smallest norm"
Among least-squares solutions:
$\|\mathbf{A}^{+}\mathbf{b}\| \le \|\mathbf{x}\|$ for every least-squares solution $\mathbf{x}$.

:::
