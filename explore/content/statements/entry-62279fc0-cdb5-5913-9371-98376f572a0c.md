::: theorem result:lanczos-local-orthogonality name="Local orthogonality of finite-precision Lanczos"
Assuming $k\varepsilon \le 1/100$, consecutive computed vectors are nearly orthogonal
(Paige), for $j < k$, <!-- value lanczosAdjacentInnerFactor j -->
$$\lvert\beta_{j+1}\,\mathbf{v}_{j+1}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{v}_{j+2}\rvert
\le 7(j + 1)\,\varepsilon\,\|\mathbf{H}\|_2 \,.$$

Note: No printed source; the maintainer's draft, `thm:local-errors`, states
$\lvert p_j\rvert \lesssim_k \varepsilon\|\mathbf{A}\|_2$ (its formalization: $1200j$).
