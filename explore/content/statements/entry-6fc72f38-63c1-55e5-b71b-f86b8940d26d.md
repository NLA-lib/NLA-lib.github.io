::: theorem result:partial-pivoting-success name="Partial pivoting succeeds exactly for nonsingular inputs"
Let $\mathbf{A}$ be a square real or complex matrix, and let $\mathbf{B}$ be the matrix
obtained by permuting the rows of $\mathbf{A}$ according to
$\mathsf{luPartialPivotPerm}(\mathbf{A})$.
All diagonal entries of $\mathbf{U}=\mathsf{luUpper}(\mathbf{B})$ are nonzero exactly
when $\mathbf{A}$ is invertible.

:::
