::: theorem result:gmres-finite-termination name="Finite termination of GMRES"
For invertible $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_\star =
\mathbf{b}$, GMRES reaches the solution once $q$ is at least the grade of
$\mathbf{r}_0 = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_0$:
$\mathsf{gmresIterate}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{x}_0, q) = \mathbf{x}_\star$.
Both cited results state that the iterate at the breakdown step, which is the grade, is exact.
:::
