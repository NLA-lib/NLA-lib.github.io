::: theorem result:rank-inequalities name="Subadditivity of rank"
The rank of a sum is at most the sum of the ranks:
$\operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}) \le \operatorname{rank} \mathbf{A} +
\operatorname{rank} \mathbf{B}$.
The cited source states this for complex matrices; here it holds over any field and finite index
types.
:::
