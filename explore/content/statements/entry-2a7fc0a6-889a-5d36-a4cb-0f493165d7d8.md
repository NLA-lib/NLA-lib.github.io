::: theorem result:schatten-norm name="The triangle inequality for Schatten norms"
For $1 \le q \le \infty$ and matrices
$\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$ of the same shape,
$\|\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}\|_{S_q} \le \|\mathbf{A}\|_{S_q} + \|\mathbf{B}\|_{S_q}$.
:::
