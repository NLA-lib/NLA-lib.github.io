::: theorem result:polar-decomposition name="A matrix with at least as many rows as columns is an isometry times its absolute value"
If $\lvert n \rvert \le \lvert m \rvert$, then
$\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{U}(\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A})^{1/2}$ for some $\mathbf{U}$ with
orthonormal columns.

:::
