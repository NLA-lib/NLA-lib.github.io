::: theorem result:pcg-recurrence name="The preconditioned Hestenes–Stiefel iterates are the PCG iterates"
For positive definite
$\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{M}$ and every $k$, with no breakdown hypothesis,
$\mathbf{x}_k = \mathsf{pcgIterate}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{M}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{x}_0, k)$.
The inherited locator requires verification.
:::
