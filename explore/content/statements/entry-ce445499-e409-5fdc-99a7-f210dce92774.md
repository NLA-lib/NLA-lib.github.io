::: theorem result:krylov-space-polynomial name="The polynomial description of the Krylov space"
Let $\Bbbk$ be the real or complex field, $\mathbf{A}$ a square matrix over
$\Bbbk$, $\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{x}$ vectors, and $q\in\mathbb{N}$. Define
$\mathcal{K}_q(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{b})=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf{b},
\mathbf{A}\mathbf{b},\ldots,\mathbf{A}^{q-1}\mathbf{b}\}$. Then
$\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{K}_q(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{b})$ exactly when there exists a
polynomial $p\in\Bbbk[X]$ of degree less than $q$ such that
$\mathbf{x}=p(\mathbf{A})\mathbf{b}$.
:::
