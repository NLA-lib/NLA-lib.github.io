::: theorem result:krylov-space-polynomial name="The polynomial description of the Krylov space"
$\mathbf{x} \in \mathcal{K}_q$ exactly
when $\mathbf{x} = p(\mathbf{A})\mathbf{b}$ for a polynomial $p \in \Bbbk[X]$ of degree less
than $q$.
:::
