::: definition name="Late-truncated GN"
First form the generalized Nyström approximation $\mathbf{B}=\mathsf{gn}(\mathbf{A},\boldsymbol{\Omega},\boldsymbol{\Psi})$. Return $\widehat{\mathbf{A}}=[\mathbf{B}]_k$, a [best rank-at-most-$k$ approximation](/explore/statements/22476dc5-4a8c-59f3-95a5-929740cd4e5b/) to the completed matrix. This truncation selects a subspace from the output rather than from the original range sketch.
:::
