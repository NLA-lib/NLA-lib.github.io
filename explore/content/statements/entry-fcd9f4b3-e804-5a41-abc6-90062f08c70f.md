::: theorem result:tape-gaussian-matrix name="A Gaussian tape draws a Gaussian matrix"
If the tape $x$ is an i.i.d. sequence of standard
Gaussians, the $N \times k$ matrix drawn from it from position $\mathit{off}$, column-major, is a
standard Gaussian matrix.

Note: No printed source.
:::
