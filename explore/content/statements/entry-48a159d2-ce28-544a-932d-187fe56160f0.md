::: theorem result:lipschitz-cfc-frobenius name="A $1$-Lipschitz function is $1$-Lipschitz in the Frobenius norm on Hermitian matrices"
For Hermitian $\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$ over $\Bbbk$ and $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with
$\lvert f(x) - f(y)\rvert \le
\lvert x - y\rvert$,
$\|f(\mathbf{A}) - f(\mathbf{B})\|_{\mathsf{F}} \le \|\mathbf{A} - \mathbf{B}\|_{\mathsf{F}}$.
This is the finite-dimensional Hermitian specialization of the cited abstract inequality, with
Lipschitz constant one. The statement includes both real and complex matrices and empty index types.
:::
