::: theorem result:cg-galerkin-formula name="CG is $\\mathbf{x}_0 + \\mathbf{Q}(\\mathbf{Q}^{\\mathsf{H}}\\mathbf{A}\\mathbf{Q})^{-1}\\mathbf{Q}^{\\mathsf{H}}\\mathbf{r}_0$ for any orthonormal basis $\\mathbf{Q}$ of the Krylov space"
For positive definite $\mathbf{A}$,

$$\mathsf{cgIterate}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{x}_0, q) = \mathbf{x}_0 + \mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{Q}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q})^{-1}\mathbf{Q}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{r}_0,\qquad \mathbf{r}_0 = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_0.$$
:::
