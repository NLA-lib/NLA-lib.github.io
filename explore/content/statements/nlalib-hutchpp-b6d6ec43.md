::: definition decl:NLAlib.hutchpp name="Hutch++"
Choose a range sketch $\mathbf{S}$ and let $\mathbf{P}$ be the [orthogonal projector](/explore/statements/2fa76a05-ce3c-5a1c-9aee-625c01bfd829/) onto $\operatorname{range}(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{S})$. Compute the captured trace $\operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{P}\mathbf{A})$ and add a [Girard–Hutchinson estimate](/explore/statements/d1ee9b33-de78-5b4f-8a2d-d80147ea5587/), with fresh probes, of $\operatorname{tr}((\mathbf{I}-\mathbf{P})\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{I}-\mathbf{P}))$. The low-rank part and residual estimator have separate roles; the probability results specify the required independence and distribution.
:::
