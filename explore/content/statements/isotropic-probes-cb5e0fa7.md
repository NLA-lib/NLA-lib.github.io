::: definition name="Isotropic probes"
A square-integrable probe $\mathbf{g}$ is isotropic when $\mathbb E[\mathbf{g}\mathbf{g}^{\mathsf{H}}]=\mathbf{I}$. [Standard real Gaussian](/explore/statements/84f513cb-f346-53d2-a25c-d47827a8967b/) and independent Rademacher probes are common examples. Isotropy specifies a second moment; it does not itself assert independent coordinates or independent probes.
:::
