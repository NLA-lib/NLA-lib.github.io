::: theorem result:positive-map-norm-at-identity name="Russo–Dye norm attainment"
A positive complex-linear map attains its largest spectral
norm on the closed spectral-norm unit ball at the identity matrix. Neither unitality nor strict
positivity of the image of the identity is required.
:::
