::: theorem result:quadratic-probe-spectral-detection name="A quadratic probe detects the spectral norm"
For a positive semidefinite matrix on a
nonempty index space there is one unit vector $\mathbf{v}$ such that every $\mathbf{z}$ satisfies
$$\|\mathbf{A}\|_2 |\mathbf{v}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{z}|^2
\le \operatorname{Re}(\mathbf{z}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{z}).$$
The first vector in an ordered orthonormal eigenbasis supplies the direction.

:::
