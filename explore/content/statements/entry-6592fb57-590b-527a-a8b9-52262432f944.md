::: theorem result:gaussian-osi name="The Gaussian OSI"
Let $k,n$ be finite index sets with $k$ nonempty, and let $\mathbf{G}$ be a random $|k|\times|n|$ matrix with independent standard real Gaussian entries. For $d\in\mathbb{N}$, the Gaussian sketch $\mathbf{S}=|k|^{-1/2}\mathbf{G}$ is an oblivious subspace injection for dimension $d$ with constant
$$\Bigl(1 - \frac{\sqrt{d} + t}{\sqrt{\lvert k \rvert}}\Bigr)^2$$
and failure probability $e^{-t^2/2}$, for $k$ nonempty and
$0 \le t \le \sqrt{\lvert k \rvert} - \sqrt{d}$.

Note: For $d > \lvert n \rvert$, the proof works at $\min(d, \lvert n \rvert)$ and weakens. The one-sided Gaussian tail gives this failure probability; the inherited definition citation is to Martinsson and Tropp.
:::
