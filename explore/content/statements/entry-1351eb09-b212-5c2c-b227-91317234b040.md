::: theorem result:bernstein-inequality name="Bernstein's inequality"
For independent sub-exponential variables: if the $X_i$ are
independent and, for $i \in s$, $X_i$ is [sub-exponential](/explore/library/declarations/NLAlib.HasSubexponentialMGF/) with parameters $(v_i, b_i)$
(`HasSubexponentialMGF`), then for $t \ge 0$
$$\mu\Bigl(\sum_{i \in s} X_i \ge t\Bigr) \le \exp\Bigl(-\min\Bigl(\frac{t^2}{2 \sum_{i \in s} v_i},
\frac{t}{2 \max_{i \in s} b_i}\Bigr)\Bigr),$$
with the convention of `measureReal_ge_le` when a denominator is $0$.
:::
