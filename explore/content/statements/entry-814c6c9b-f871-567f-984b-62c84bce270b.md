::: theorem result:haar-sketching-identity name="The random orthonormal sketching identity"
Let $m,p,r,s,d\in\mathbb{N}$, with $p\le m$, $r+s=m$ and $r+2\le p$.
Let $\mathbf{Q}\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times p}$ be a random Haar frame, and let
$\mathbf{V}_1\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times r}$ and $\mathbf{V}_2\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times s}$
be fixed orthonormal frames with $\mathbf{V}_1^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{V}_2=\mathbf{0}$.
Then, for every $\mathbf{B}\in\mathbb{R}^{s\times d}$,
$$\mathbb{E}\|(\mathbf{Q}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{V}_1)^{+}\mathbf{Q}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{V}_2\mathbf{B}
\|_{\mathsf{F}}^2 = \frac{m-p}{m-r}\cdot\frac{r}{p-r-1}\,\|\mathbf{B}\|_{\mathsf{F}}^2\,,$$

Note: This is the Haar counterpart of `NLAlib.integral_frobNorm_pinv_mul_mul_sq_of_hasLaw_gaussianMatrix`, smaller than
the Gaussian value $r/(p-r-1)$ by the factor $(m-p)/(m-r)$. Unlike the Gaussian case the
frames must be complementary: the proof uses
$\mathbf{\Omega}_1^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{\Omega}_1 + \mathbf{\Omega}_2^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{\Omega}_2 =
\mathbf{Q}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{Q} = \mathbf{I}$ for the blocks
$\mathbf{\Omega}_i = \mathbf{V}_i^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{Q}$.
The cited proposition gives an inequality for general $\mathbf{R}$ and equality at
$\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{I}$ over $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{C}$; this statement is the real case at
$\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{I}$.
:::
