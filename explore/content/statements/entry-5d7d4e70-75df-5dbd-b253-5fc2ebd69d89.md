::: theorem result:lanczos-fa-exactness name="Exactness of Lanczos-FA"
for Hermitian $\mathbf{A}$ and
$\nu \le q$, $\mathsf{lanczosFA}(\mathbf{A}, f, \mathbf{b}, q) = f(\mathbf{A})\mathbf{b}$, since
the Krylov space is then invariant.
:::
