::: theorem result:tomic-weyl-inequality name="Tomić–Weyl"
If $\mathbf{x}$ is weakly majorized by $\mathbf{y}$ and $\varphi$ is monotone
and convex on $\mathbb{R}$, then $\sum_i \varphi(\mathbf{x}_i) \le \sum_i \varphi(\mathbf{y}_i)$.
:::
