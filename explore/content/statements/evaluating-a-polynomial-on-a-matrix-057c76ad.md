::: definition name="Evaluating a polynomial on a matrix"
For $p(t)=\sum_{j=0}^k c_jt^j$, set $p(\mathbf{A})=\sum_{j=0}^k c_j\mathbf{A}^j$. Applying $p(\mathbf{A})$ to $\mathbf{b}$ produces a vector in the Krylov space generated by $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{b}$. For Hermitian $\mathbf{A}$, [functional calculus](/explore/statements/af61ef21-9f45-5d71-87c8-6d01c16be15e/) evaluates a scalar function on its real eigenvalues.
:::
