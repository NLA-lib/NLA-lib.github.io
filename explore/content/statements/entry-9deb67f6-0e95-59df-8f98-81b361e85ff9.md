::: theorem result:loewner-heinz name="The logarithm is operator monotone"
On positive definite matrices, the logarithm preserves the Loewner order: if $\mathbf{A}$ is
positive definite and $\mathbf{A} \preceq \mathbf{B}$, then
$\log(\mathbf{A}) \preceq \log(\mathbf{B})$.

:::
