::: definition name="Krylov space and grade"
For $\mathbf{A}\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ and $\mathbf{b}\in\mathbb R^n$, define $\mathcal K_k(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{b})=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf{b},\mathbf{A}\mathbf{b},\ldots,\mathbf{A}^{k-1}\mathbf{b}\}$. The grade is the first index at which this sequence of subspaces stops growing.
:::
