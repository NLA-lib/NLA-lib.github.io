::: theorem result:wielandt-inequality name="The Wielandt inequality"
Let $n$ be a finite index set, $\Bbbk$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and
$\mathbf{A}\in\Bbbk^{n\times n}$ be Hermitian. If $a\mathbf{I} \preceq \mathbf{A} \preceq b\mathbf{I}$,
$0 < a$, and $\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}\in\Bbbk^n$ are orthogonal, then
$$\lvert \mathbf{x}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{y} \rvert^2
  \le \Bigl(\frac{b - a}{b + a}\Bigr)^2
  \operatorname{Re}(\mathbf{x}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x})
  \operatorname{Re}(\mathbf{y}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{y})\,.$$
:::
