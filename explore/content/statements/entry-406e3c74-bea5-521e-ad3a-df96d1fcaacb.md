::: theorem result:complementary-nullities name="Complementary nullities"
For an invertible $\mathbf{A}$ and sets of indices $r$ (rows) and
$c$ (columns), the block of $\mathbf{A}$ on rows $r$ and columns $c$ and the block of
$\mathbf{A}^{-1}$ on rows outside $c$ and columns outside $r$ have kernels of the same dimension.
The blocks need not be square. This holds over any field.
:::
