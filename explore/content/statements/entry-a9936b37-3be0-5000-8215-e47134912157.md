::: theorem result:projector-gap name="The gap between two orthogonal projectors"
$\|\mathbf{P} - \mathbf{Q}\|_2$ is the larger
of the two directed gaps, $\max(\|(\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{Q})\mathbf{P}\|_2, \|(\mathbf{I} -
\mathbf{P})\mathbf{Q}\|_2)$.
:::
