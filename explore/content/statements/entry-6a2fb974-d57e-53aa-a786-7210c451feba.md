::: theorem result:stein-equation name="Stein equation, unique solvability"
For $\rho(\mathbf{A})<1$ and every
forcing $\mathbf{Q}$, there is exactly one $\mathbf{X}$ satisfying
$\mathbf{X}-\mathbf{A}\mathbf{X}\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}=\mathbf{Q}$.
Boyd’s slide account treats real matrices with symmetric forcing. The present statement allows
arbitrary forcing over `RCLike`.
Adapted from `tchen-research/NLAlib` commit `77514ce42a0e437d837797a063ac161f41022730`.
:::
