::: theorem result:chebyshev-coeff-analytic-decay name="Chebyshev coefficient decay for analytic functions"
A compatible extension holomorphic
and bounded by $M$ on the OPEN Bernstein ellipse gives
$$\|a_k\|\le\frac{2M}{\rho^k}\quad(k>0).$$
The coefficient bound is generalized to RCLike values via the compatible complex extension.
The zero coefficient has the more general bound `norm_chebyshevCoeff_zero_le`.

Note: The proof takes smaller radii up to $\rho$; no outer-boundary continuity is assumed.
:::
