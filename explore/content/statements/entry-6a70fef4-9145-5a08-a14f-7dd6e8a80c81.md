::: theorem result:trace-of-matrix-function name="The trace of $f(\\mathbf{A})$"
is the sum of $f$ over the eigenvalues: for Hermitian
$\mathbf{A}$,
$\operatorname{Re} \operatorname{tr} f(\mathbf{A})
= \sum_{i < \lvert n \rvert} f(\lambda_i(\mathbf{A}))$.
In the cited definition, the sum defines the trace function, with the remark that it agrees with
the trace of the standard matrix function.
:::
