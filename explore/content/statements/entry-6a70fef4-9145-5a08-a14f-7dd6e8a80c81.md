::: theorem result:trace-of-matrix-function name="The trace of $f(\\mathbf{A})$"
Let $n$ be a finite index type, let $\mathbb{K}\in\{\mathbb{R},\mathbb{C}\}$, let $\mathbf{A}$ be a Hermitian matrix over $\mathbb{K}$ indexed by $n$, and let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$. The real trace of the matrix function is the sum of $f$ over the eigenvalues, with $|n|$ denoting the dimension:

$$\operatorname{Re} \operatorname{tr} f(\mathbf{A}) = \sum_{i < \lvert n \rvert} f(\lambda_i(\mathbf{A}))\,. $$

Note: The cited definition gives this sum as the trace function and notes that it agrees with the trace of the standard matrix function.
:::
