::: theorem result:krylov-space-dimension name="Dimension of the Krylov space"
$\dim\mathcal{K}_q(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{b}) = \min(q, \nu)$,
$\nu$ the grade of $\mathbf{b}$ for $\mathbf{A}$. In Saad’s notation, this is equation (6.4).
:::
