::: theorem result:stein-series-truncation name="Stein series truncation, norm bound"
If $\|\mathbf{A}\|_2\le q<1$, then every unitarily
invariant
norm satisfies
$$\left\|\sum_{j\ge0}\mathbf{A}^j\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}})^j\right\|_{\Phi}
\le \frac{\|\mathbf{Q}\|_{\Phi}}{1-q^2}\,.$$

Note: No printed source. Generalizes the spectral and Frobenius estimates of `tchen-research/NLAlib`
commit `77514ce42a0e437d837797a063ac161f41022730`.
:::
