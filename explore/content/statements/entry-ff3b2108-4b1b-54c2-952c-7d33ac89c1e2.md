::: theorem result:normal-equations name="The normal equations"
$\mathbf{x}$ is a least-squares solution exactly when
$\mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{A}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{b}$.

:::
