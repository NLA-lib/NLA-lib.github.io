::: theorem result:chebyshev-growth name="Radical closed form of Chebyshev polynomials"
For $x\ge1$,
$$T_q(x)=\frac{(x+\sqrt{x^2-1})^q+(x-\sqrt{x^2-1})^q}{2}.$$
The reciprocal term is rewritten algebraically.
:::
