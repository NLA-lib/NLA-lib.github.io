::: theorem result:trace-convexity name="A convex function gives a convex trace function"
For $f$ convex on $s$, the function
$\mathbf{A} \mapsto \operatorname{Re} \operatorname{tr} f(\mathbf{A})$ is convex on the Hermitian
matrices with spectrum in $s$.
:::
