::: theorem result:bernstein-ellipse-approx name="Polynomial approximation of analytic functions on an interval"
Let $\mathbb{K}$ be a real or complex scalar field, $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{K}$, $F:\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$, and $a<b$, $\rho>1$. Let $N\in\mathbb{N}$. Suppose $F$ is holomorphic and bounded in absolute value by $M$ on the [open Bernstein ellipse of parameter $\rho$](/explore/library/declarations/NLAlib.openBernsteinEllipse/), and agrees on $[-1,1]$ with the complex extension of the affine pullback of $f$:

$$F(t)=\iota_{\mathbb{K}}\!\left(f\!\left(\frac{(b-a)t+b+a}{2}\right)\right),\qquad -1\le t\le1,$$
where $\iota_{\mathbb{K}}$ is the canonical inclusion into $\mathbb{C}$.

Then the canonical truncation gives a polynomial $p$ of degree at most $N$ whose uniform error on $[a,b]$ is at most
$$\frac{2M}{\rho^N(\rho-1)}\,.$$ This includes $N=0$.

Note: This is an affine-rescaling corollary generalized to RCLike values.
:::
