::: theorem result:bernstein-ellipse-approx name="Polynomial approximation of analytic functions on an interval"
On $[a,b]$ with $a<b$,
a compatible extension of the affine pullback of $f$, holomorphic
and bounded by $M$ on the [open Bernstein ellipse](/explore/library/declarations/NLAlib.openBernsteinEllipse/), gives a polynomial of degree at most $N$
with uniform error
$$\frac{2M}{\rho^N(\rho-1)}\,.$$
It is an affine-rescaling corollary, generalized to RCLike values.
The polynomial is obtained from the canonical truncation, including $N=0$.
:::
