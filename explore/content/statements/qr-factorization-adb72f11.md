::: definition name="QR factorization"
For $\mathbf{A}\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$ with $m\ge n$, a thin QR factorization is $\mathbf{A}=\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{R}$, where $\mathbf{Q}^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf{Q}=\mathbf{I}$ and $\mathbf{R}$ is upper triangular. Full QR extends $\mathbf{Q}$ to a unitary basis. Rank deficiency permits zero diagonal entries of $\mathbf{R}$. Householder transformations, Givens rotations, and [Gram–Schmidt](/explore/statements/47fa9a99-bf7f-54d6-ad4e-a307c2efa85a/) are different ways to compute factors; QR eigenvalue iteration is a separate algorithm.
:::
