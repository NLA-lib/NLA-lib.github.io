::: definition name="Late-truncated Nyström subspace iteration"
For Hermitian positive-semidefinite $\mathbf{A}$, first form $\mathbf{B}=\mathsf{nyssi}(\mathbf{A},\boldsymbol{\Omega},p)=\mathsf{nystrom}(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{A}^p\boldsymbol{\Omega})$. Return $[\mathbf{B}]_r$, a best rank-at-most-$r$ approximation to $\mathbf{B}$; here $[\cdot]_r$ denotes the [rank-$r$ truncated singular-value decomposition](/explore/statements/22476dc5-4a8c-59f3-95a5-929740cd4e5b/).
:::
