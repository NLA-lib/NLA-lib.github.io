::: theorem result:leverage-sampling-ose name="Row sampling under leverage domination"
If $\ell_i(\mathbf{U}) \le L p_i$ for every row,
the row-sampling sketch with $k$ samples is a subspace embedding for
$\operatorname{range}\mathbf{U}$ with constants $1 \mp \varepsilon$ outside probability
$$2 \operatorname{rank}\mathbf{U} \cdot e^{-\varepsilon^2 k / (3L)} \,,$$
for $0 \le \varepsilon \le 1$ and any $\mathbf{U}$. Woodruff's cited Theorem 17 assumes $\mathbf{U}$
with orthonormal columns, $p$ a $\beta$-approximation of the leverage-score distribution and
$s > 144 d \log(2d/\delta) / (\beta \varepsilon^2)$ samples through a Bernstein bound (Fact 1). Here the result uses matrix Chernoff, $L = d/\beta$, and allows any shape and rank;
the other cited sources discuss this matrix-Chernoff context.
:::
