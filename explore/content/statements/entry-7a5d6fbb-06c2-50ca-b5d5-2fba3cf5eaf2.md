::: theorem result:coefficient-block-partial-transpose name="Attainment for commuting coefficients"
For a finite pairwise commuting Hermitian
family, $\sqrt{\lambda_0(\mathbf{C})} = \sqrt{\|\sum_i \mathbf{A}_i^2\|_2}$.

:::
