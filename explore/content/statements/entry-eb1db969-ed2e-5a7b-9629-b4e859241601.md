::: theorem result:klein-inequality name="Klein's inequality"
For $f$ convex on $s$ with derivative $f'$ within $s$ at every point
of $s$, and [Hermitian](/explore/statements/49f5c3f6-f926-5bc6-b401-16993ec1e357/) $\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$ with spectrum in $s$,
$$\operatorname{Re} \operatorname{tr}((\mathbf{A} - \mathbf{B}) f'(\mathbf{B}))
  \le \operatorname{Re} \operatorname{tr} f(\mathbf{A}) - \operatorname{Re} \operatorname{tr}
  f(\mathbf{B}) \,.$$
:::
