::: theorem result:subexponential-tail name="The sub-exponential tail"
if $X$ has variance proxy $v$ and scale $b$, then for $t \ge 0$
$$\mu(X \ge t) \le \exp\Bigl(-\min\Bigl(\frac{t^2}{2v}, \frac{t}{2b}\Bigr)\Bigr)\,.$$ If $b = 0$ or $v = 0$, Lean's division by zero makes the
exponent $0$ and the bound $1$; the regime forms are the usable statements then (for a
sub-Gaussian variable, $b = 0$, use `measureReal_ge_le_of_mul_le`).
:::
