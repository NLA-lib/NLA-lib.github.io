::: theorem result:orthogonal-polynomial-zeros name="The zeros of the orthogonal polynomial are real"
If $\mu$, finite and supported in
$[a, b]$, is positive definite up to degree $k$, then $\pi_k$ has $k$ real zeros counted with
multiplicity. The inherited theorem locators require verification.
:::
