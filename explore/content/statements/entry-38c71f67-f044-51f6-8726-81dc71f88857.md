::: theorem result:lanczos-local-errors name="Local error bound for finite-precision Lanczos"
Assuming $k\varepsilon \le 1/100$, the computed vectors
satisfy the three-term recurrence up to a small residual, for $j < k$, <!-- value lanczosRecurrenceErrorFactor j -->
$$\|\mathbf{H}\mathbf{v}_{j+1} - \beta_j\mathbf{v}_j - \alpha_{j+1}\mathbf{v}_{j+1} -
\beta_{j+1}\mathbf{v}_{j+2}\| \le 6(j + 1)\,\varepsilon\,\|\mathbf{H}\|_2 \,.$$

Note: No printed source; the maintainer's draft, `thm:local-errors`, states
$\|\mathbf{f}_j\| \lesssim_k \varepsilon\|\mathbf{A}\|_2$ (its formalization: $700j$); the
constant here comes from the sharper coefficient bounds. Paige's cited analysis gives bounds of this kind directly from floating-point arithmetic.
:::
