::: definition decl:NLAlib.rsvd name="Range-finder approximation"
The untruncated approximation is $\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{P}_{\mathbf{Y}}\mathbf{A}$. Its rank is at most the sketch width; a target rank $k$ is not imposed at this stage.
