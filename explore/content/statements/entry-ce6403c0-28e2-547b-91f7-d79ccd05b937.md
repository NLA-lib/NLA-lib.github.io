::: theorem result:sudakov-fernique name="The Sudakov–Fernique inequality"
for centred finite Gaussian families
$(X_t)_{t \in T}$, $(Y_t)_{t \in T}$ with $\mathbb{E}(X_s - X_t)^2 \le \mathbb{E}(Y_s - Y_t)^2$
for all $s, t$, $\mathbb{E}\max_t X_t \le \mathbb{E}\max_t Y_t$.
Vershynin states this without an equal-variance hypothesis (unlike Slepian's inequality), and
the two families may be on different probability spaces.
:::
