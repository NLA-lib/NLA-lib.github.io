::: theorem result:best-approximation-uniqueness name="Every best approximation is the chosen one"
For $a < b$, $f$ continuous on $[a, b]$ and
$p$ a real polynomial of degree at most $n$ with $\lvert f - p \rvert \le E_n(f; [a, b])$ on
$[a, b]$, $p = \mathrm{minimaxPoly}\ f\ [a, b]\ n$.
:::
