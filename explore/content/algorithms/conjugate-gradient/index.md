## Mathematical CG in exact arithmetic

Let $A$ be symmetric positive definite, let $Ax_\star=b$, and write $x_k$ for the mathematical CG iterate from $x_0$. The energy norm is $\|v\|_A^2=v^{\mathsf T}Av$.

::: theorem result:cg-optimality name="Energy optimality"
The mathematical CG iterate minimizes the energy error over the initial guess plus its Krylov space:

$$x_k\in\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{\mathbf y\in\mathbf x_0+\mathcal K_k(\mathbf A,\mathbf r_0)}\|x_\star-\mathbf y\|_A^2.$$

Here $\mathcal K_k(\mathbf A,\mathbf r_0)=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf r_0,\mathbf A\mathbf r_0,\ldots,\mathbf A^{k-1}\mathbf r_0\}$. The energy error cannot increase as the search space grows.
:::

::: theorem result:cg-chebyshev-bound name="Chebyshev convergence"
If the eigenvalues of $A$ lie in $[a,a^{\prime}]$, $a>0$, then

$$\|x_\star-x_k\|_A^2\le4\left(\frac{\sqrt\kappa-1}{\sqrt\kappa+1}\right)^{2k}\|x_\star-x_0\|_A^2,\qquad\kappa=\frac{a^{\prime}}a.$$

The positive spectral interval controls the rate. The implementation pages connect this mathematical result to stored solver outputs.
:::

::: theorem result:cg-polynomial-bound name="Polynomial error bound"
For every polynomial $p$ of degree at most $k$ with $p(0)=1$,

$$\|x_\star-x_k\|_A^2\le\|p(A)(x_\star-x_0)\|_A^2.$$

A polynomial small on the spectrum therefore yields a small CG error.
:::

::: theorem result:cg-finite-termination name="Finite termination"
Let $g$ be the dimension of the Krylov space generated by the initial residual. Once $k\ge g$, the mathematical iterate satisfies $x_k=x_\star$. In dimension $n$, at most $n$ exact-arithmetic steps suffice.
:::
