## Definition

::: definition name="LSQR"
Use Golub–Kahan bidiagonalization of $\mathbf A$ and the initial residual to form successive small least-squares problems. Lift their solutions into the growing right search space. For zero start, that space is generated by $\mathbf A^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf b$ under $\mathbf A^{\mathsf{H}}\mathbf A$. Its exact-arithmetic relationship to CGLS does not identify their rounded computations.
:::
